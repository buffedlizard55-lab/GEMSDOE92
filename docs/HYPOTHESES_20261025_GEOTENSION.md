## 2. Named Non-Fault Process That Can Mimic Rank-1 (`GEOTENSION`)

Even after `H_ten` gates to thin-cover piedmont (cover 20–600 m) and coherent scarp ridges, **three non-fault processes** can still mimic `GEOTENSION`:

1. **Distributed alluvial compaction / subsidence bowls** (e.g., Clayton Valley lithium-brine pumping, agricultural groundwater drawdown, playa desiccation) producing apparent positive dilatation and second-invariant highs without brittle fault slip. A high-`H_ten` dot in deep basin interior (`d15 > 1000 m`) may be poroelastic compaction, not a fault.
2. **Elastic strain shadows and interseismic locking gradients** around locked Walker Lane dextral strike-slip segments (e.g., Pyramid Lake fault) where geodetic shear/dilatation is high but permeability is shear-parallel, not tensile-open.
3. **Incised alluvial-fan terrace risers / entrenched wash walls** that create coherent `R_scarp` slope-breaks across piedmont aprons without tectonic slip—indistinguishable from a fan-washed fault scarp in DEM alone (requires strain gating to suppress, but `H_ten` is smooth at ~10 km GPS posting and can leak).

## 3. Pre-Registered 3-Experiment Design & Decision Rule

All three budgeted experiments use the **single frozen `GEOTENSION` surface `S_raw(x)`** (`gems/geotension.py`), zero catalogue inputs in `S_raw`, exact unit-mass budget `N = 37,654` dots (`d_cat > 2.0 px`, zero catalogue mass), varying only the structural skeleton coupling fraction along a clean 3-point ablation:

1. **Experiment 1 (`s60f40`)**: `22,592` dots (`60.0%`) emitted across the **full eligible `H19-5` network** (`h19 & valid & d_cat > 2.0`, not restricted to `R0`'s scan-order subset) via exact marginal expected-DTI greedy (`scripts/exact_dti_refill.py`) under `w_skel = H_ten · (0.25 + 0.75·S_pct)` on `h19` (`0.10·S_raw` off `h19`), plus `15,062` far dots (`40.0%`) emitted via `spaced_top_mask(S_raw, min_dist_px=2.8)` on 1D ridge crests strictly `> 3.0 px` off `H19-5`.
2. **Experiment 2 (`s50f50`)**: `18,827` dots (`50.0%`) on `H19-5` via exact marginal DTI greedy + `18,827` far dots (`50.0%`) on 1D `GEOTENSION` crests `> 3.0 px` off `H19-5`.
3. **Experiment 3 (`free1d`)**: `37,654` dots (`100.0%` standalone) emitted directly on `valid & ridge_1d & (d_cat > 2.0)` via `spaced_top_mask(S_raw, min_dist_px=2.8)` — **zero use of `R0` or `H19-5`**.

### Promotion Gate (Fail-Closed)

A built artifact may receive `verdict: "promote"` **only if ALL four conditions hold simultaneously**:
1. **Holdout Best Gate:** Pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, 22,663 withheld positives, 8 buffered spatial folds) strictly exceeds the repository's current holdout best (`APRONRIDGE free1d = 0.008354`), with 95% paired spatial-cluster bootstrap CI excluding `0` against `R0` (and paired comparison reported against `APRONRIDGE free1d`, `HYDROCONDUIT f45hydro`, `RELIEF p10`, `STRAINBREAK f60s2i`).
2. **Leakage Canary Gate:** Every individual input band and composite surface has symmetric AUC `≤ 0.90` on withheld positives.
3. **Literal Uniqueness Gate (Both Phases & Scopes):** Forward 3-px overlap `≤ 0.70` and `|Spearman ρ| ≤ 0.90` on **both** the pre-placement surface and the final placed dots against **every** local registry raster **and** against the binding public registry check. *Note:* Under the literal public-witness rule as currently coded (`vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py`, no density exemption), `17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale` (100% footprint positive, 5,167,373 cells) necessarily triggers `unique_under_literal_rule: false` / `stop_required: true` for any nonempty in-footprint raster. This project never waives that witness silently; if `GEOTENSION` clears Gates 1,2,4 and local uniqueness, it is published as the new primary **locally holdout-best & locally lane-unique research GeoTIFF**, while keeping `submission_eligible: false` and `verdict: "negative"` under the binding literal rule. A companion fix (density-exempt `>150k` px) is proposed in `IRREGULARITIES.md` for operator review but is not applied unilaterally.
4. **Automated Format Gate:** 10/10 MUST gates pass on independent disk re-read (`EPSG:32611`, `3730 × 3292`, template geotransform, `float32`, single band, `nodata=None`, all 12,279,160 cells finite in `[0, 1]`, zeros outside footprint, zero catalogue mass).
