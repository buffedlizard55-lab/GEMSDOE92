Why might GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 have scored well?

The plausible mechanism is prediction geometry, not a proven causal explanation. The R0 raster is a sparse dot set derived from Poisson-disk thinning of a candidate linework surface, with a catalogue-distance collar that excludes close parallel predictions. Under a distance-tolerant score, thinning can reduce redundant false-positive mass while retaining nearby coverage, and catalogue masking can suppress predictions on already-known faults. The exact point count, feature set, and thresholding could affect that tradeoff.

However, the archived static HOLDOUT-DTI for the GEMSDOE32 raster is low and does not validate the reported leaderboard result. Hidden faults are not known in local validation, the site score has no receipt, and multiple focus-site rasters are exact clones or near-clones. Therefore we cannot claim that a particular filtering choice caused 0.2778, that the value is reproducible, or that any site result beats the current leaderboard.
