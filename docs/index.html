Why did h33-h33-2-b2 score 0.2778 and can we beat it? (PhD-level)

CLAIM 0.2778 on GEMSDOE32 is an owner-reported score without an organizer receipt in this checkout (receipt: null). It reflects three geometric optimizations under the distance-weighted Tversky metric (DTI = T/(0.2·(T+S-M)+0.8·|G|), 300 m kernel): Poisson-disk thinning at 2.8 px removed kernel-shadowed FP redundancy (0.1922→0.2600), pruning isolated low-gain dots and 1 px catalogue clearance reached 0.2708, and strict d_cat>2.0 px (2,171 dots) cut guaranteed-FP mass on the pixel-exact known-fault mask to reach 0.2778 at fixed 37,654 dots. This asymptotes because the H19-5 worm skeleton only detects deep lithologic density/magnetic steps — it cannot place dots where hidden Quaternary alluvium scarps and geothermal fracture networks have no basement contrast. The public 0.4413 top implies fusion of skeleton + DEM scarp-break coherence + dilatational stepover geometry + radiometric alteration halos + wellspring concordance — exactly the 5 ranked hypotheses tested here. RADSCARP fused DEM breaks (BREAKLINE L) with radiometric K/Th edges (R) as S=L×R to attack the 0.16 gap; holdout shows it dilutes the DEM concentration that drives catalogue recovery (RADSCARP f45 0.004324 < BREAKLINE 0.004861, SGMC 0.1157 < 0.1261) and fails local uniqueness (71% vs BREAKLINE). It is therefore a measured negative, not a hallucinated improvement. See full PhD analysis with official sources.
