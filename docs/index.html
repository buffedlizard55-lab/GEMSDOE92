What might explain the reported 0.2778 — and can this beat it?

The supplied GEMSDOE32 lineage is consistent with a fixed-budget ridge candidate set, spatial thinning to reduce redundant hits, pruning low-value mass, and avoiding known traces. Under the asymmetric distance-weighted Tversky score, a well-placed, spatially distinct dot can credit nearby hidden truth, while redundant or distant mass adds false-positive cost. This is a plausible mechanistic explanation, not a proven causal account: this checkout lacks the exact organizer receipt, private truth, and confirmation that the listed bytes were the evaluated artifact.
