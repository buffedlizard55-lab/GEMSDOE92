⚠ The portal error “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” — fixed here

The portal validates every cell including outside the study-area footprint. Two historical failures produce the same message:

  • Sentinel leak: training_features.tif encodes nodata as −3.4028235e+38 on 7,113,308 cells, of which 3,061 lie inside the footprint. Writing them through puts ~0.06 % of inside pixels outside [0,1].
  • NaN leak: A raster with nodata=NaN but a stray NaN inside the footprint also fails the range check.

Fix verified here: every download is all-finite — we write with nodata=None, outside = 0.0 (finite), inside = 0.0/1.0, then re-read and assert n_violations=0 min=0.0 max=1.0 nan_pixels=0. It descends from H19-5 (121 k-px power-law ridge) → D1.5 → D2.8 (44,090 Poisson-disk thinned) → H27-4-SOLO (40,199) → prune B2 (remove ≤2 px from catalogue) → 37,654. Why it won (PhD-level read): DTI is a budget: DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2(S−M) + 0.8|G|). Each dot pays +1 to S; it only helps if its marginal kernel credit c > 0.2·DTI ≈ 0.056 at DTI 0.28. H19-5 is a thick surface (121 k px) — most of its pixels are redundant cover of the same truth. D2.8 thins it while preserving geometry: it deletes mass that was already credited, raising credit per dot from ~0.031 to ~0.054 and sitting near the break-even bar 0.2·0.26 = 0.052 derived from the formula (independent empirical inversion gave 0.0548 — same answer two ways). The prune B2 then deletes the 4–6 % of dots that sit within 200 m of known faults where the catalogue is masked and T cannot be earned.

Can we beat 0.2778 with DILATATION? At the same budget S=37,654, beating it requires raising mean credit per dot by ~25 % (more of the 37,654 must sit within 300 m of hidden truth). The catalogue cannot supply that — every new public compilation except one pixel already lies within 300 m of it. The only path is a better detector for the hidden set: not “where are there ridges?” but “which ridges are currently opening?” Dilatation maps that. Holdout says DILATATION p12 gains HOLDOUT-DTI +0.00177 [0.00111, 0.00246] over R0 on 22,663 withheld catalogue segments (p<0.05, 8 spatial folds) — same instrument that gave RELIEF +0.00197. That is not a score, but it is the pre-registered reason to spend a weekly slot.

Hypothesis, mechanism, non-fault mimic, prediction

Hypothesis

Geodetic dilatation rate (Band 8 geod_dilaterate, nstrain/yr) measures volumetric opening of the crust. In the Great Basin, ~32 % of geothermal systems occupy dilatational step-overs/relay ramps and another ~25 % at terminations/intersections (Faulds et al. 2006, 2010, 2021a; Giddens & Faulds 2025). The catalogue maps Quaternary scarps; the strain field maps where those scarps are currently opening. A blind fault under 10–100 m of fan alluvium has no scarp but still dilates. Shifting 12 % of dots from contractive/low-dilatation ground into the high-dilatation tercile (≥7.24 nstrain/yr) on H19-5 ridge support increases the chance that a dot’s 300 m kernel is centered on an unmapped, actively dilating fault.

Mechanism

DTI pays per dot only if its credit exceeds 0.2·DTI. At fixed S=37,654 we keep only 4,518 dots that sit where the 19-band stack already says “plausible” (0.40·TMI + 0.35·grav + 0.25·scarp) weighted by 0.70·dilatation + 0.30·geophysics on ridges, and select them by greedy exact expected-DTI marginal gain (α 0.2 β 0.8 R 3 px) strictly inside the high-dilatation tercile, on H19-5, d_cat>2.0 px, off catalogue. Each moved dot replaces a dot whose expected credit was near zero.

Named non-fault process that could mimic it

Alluvial subsidence bowls from lithium-brine pumping (Clayton Valley) and agricultural drawdown, plus elastic strain shadows around locked Walker Lane strike-slip segments and ductile stretching of thick basin fill (>1,500 m to basement, Band 15) can produce high dilatation without brittle Quaternary fault permeability. A high-dilatation dot in a deep basin interior may be thick-fill stretching, not a fault.

Irregularities & limitations — we flag instead of hide

  • Parent R0 itself has 2,171 dots within 3 px of the catalogue (5.77 %); any non-PRUNE descendant inherits that — we enforce d_cat>2.0 on moved dots and record the inherited collar (IRREGULARITIES.md 2026-10-23).
  • Previous MASS ranking was invalid — H19-5 is binary {0,1} with 1.0 at every R0 dot, so “ranking” was a tie (IRREGULARITIES 2026-10-23 PRUNE session).
  • Competition rasters are in a public repo — flagged per brief constraint 2, human must confirm data-use terms.
  • Holdout uses catalogue positives, not hidden truth — it cannot reward a genuinely new fault; we treat |Δ|<0.01 as no evidence and require a live receipt for decisions.
  • SGMC control is negative for DILATATION (−0.012 to −0.016) — expected: SGMC bedrock faults live in range cores, not dilating piedmonts.
  • Budget: 1 axis (DILATATION) × 3 doses this session = 3 experiments, within the brief’s 3-experiment / 2-hour cap; weekly promotion is a separate selector step.
