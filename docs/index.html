This session's lane: DILATATION (geodetic volumetric strain; comparison lane, not primary download)

Hypothesis. Band 8 geod_dilaterate is a kinematic field: the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue maps Quaternary scarps, while strain maps where those scarps are currently opening. Faulds et al. put ~32 % of Great Basin geothermal systems in dilatational step-overs / relay ramps and ~25 % at terminations and intersections (OSTI 1110517, Giddens & Faulds 2025). A blind fault under 10–100 m of fan alluvium has no scarp to map but still dilates — which is exactly the Phase-2 target of newly mapped geometry that the catalogue cannot supply.

Mechanism. Hold count at 37,654. Move 12 % (4,518) of the lowest-dilatation parent dots into the high tercile (≥ 7.24 nstrain/yr) on H19-5 support, placed by greedy exact expected-DTI (α 0.2, β 0.8, R 300 m) under a prior of 0.70·dilatation + 0.30·TMI/grav/scarp. Catalogue mass stays zero; d_cat > 2.0 px on every dot.

Named non-fault mimic. Alluvial subsidence bowls from lithium-brine pumping (Clayton Valley) and agricultural drawdown; elastic strain shadows around locked Walker Lane strike-slip segments; ductile stretching of thick basin fill (> 1,500 m to basement) without brittle faulting.

Why parent 0.2778 is high — and what beating it requires

h33-h33-2-b2 (GEMSDOE32) is an owner-reported CLAIM 0.2778, not an organizer receipt. The climb that produced it is mechanical, not mysterious:

  1. H19-5 multiline ridges ~0.1922 CLAIM — geophysical lineaments already sit near hidden truth.
  2. Poisson-disk thinning at 2.8 px → 0.2600 CLAIM — DTI uses α = 0.2, β = 0.8, so extra mass is expensive. Unit-mass dots near the 300 m kernel diameter stop double-counting the same truth pixel.
  3. SOLO prune → 0.2708 CLAIM — drop dots whose credit is below ~0.2·DTI ≈ 0.056.
  4. B2 catalogue collar → 0.2778 CLAIM — known USGS/INGENIOUS traces are masked at scoring, so dots on them add S without T. Organizer forum 11516.

At a fixed budget of 37,654 dots, beating it needs ~25 % more mean credit per dot. No public catalogue can supply that. The only route is a better detector — which is what a kinematic prior is for.

A public leaderboard number of 0.4413 / 0.3195 (prompt-supplied, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED in this repo) is a different chunk mix. Nothing on this page is an organizer score.
