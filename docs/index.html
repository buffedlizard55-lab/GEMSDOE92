What was tested — SIGNED-GAMMA

Hypothesis: Blind basin-bounding faults under cover juxtapose high-density bedrock against low-density basin fill, making a gravity step. The isostatic HGM crest sits over the basement edge; for a dipping/listric fault the surface scarp is 300–500 m basinward (depth × cot(dip)). The Qfaults/INGENIOUS catalogue (older aerial imagery, pre-2020) mapped the scarp only where visible; H19-5 worms required magnetic+gravity concordance and missed pure density steps. Expert DEM mapping (live label source) + gravity inversion together trace the offset.

Mechanism: HGM ridge = Band 5 pct>85 (top 15% gravity gradients). L = pct_rank(Band 5) × pct_rank(|Band 18|) (strong magnitude + strong E-W step). Far pool = pixels 3–5 px basinward of ridges (dx = −sign(Band18)×[3,4,5]), i.e. east where Band 18 <0 (gravity falling east → basin east), west where >0. Keep 20,710 parent dots via exact expected-DTI greedy under w=0.30+0.70·L_pct on H19-5 support (coverage-aware ranking); emit 16,944 far dots via spaced_top_mask descending L_raw, 2.8 px floor, >3 px off every H19-5 pixel, >2 px off catalogue, valid topography. Total 37,654, zero catalogue mass, d_cat>2.
