Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778

R0 is D2.8 ∧ d_cat > 2: the H19-5 multiline skeleton dotted every 2.8 px (mean on-trace credit 1 − s/12 ≈ 0.77, with 2.7× fewer positives), then with every dot within 200 m of a mapped fault removed. Those removals gained exactly the pure-false-positive amount per dot (2.78×10⁻⁶ and 2.75×10⁻⁶), so there is almost no new-fault truth beside mapped traces. Limitations and next steps

  • The estimate is a MODEL fitted to CLAIM scores from the public leaderboard subset. The private set and Phase 2 use other or revised labels, so generalisation is not guaranteed.
  • The candidate concentrates dots more strongly than any scored raster did. The trust-region cap holds the consensus share at ≤ 55% (scored rasters reached 49%), but extrapolation error is not fully captured by the bootstrap.
  • Uniqueness uses interpretation PI-1. Under the literal reading, a raster positive on every footprint cell makes every candidate a duplicate (reported in the run card).
  • Next: submit once and record the organizer score in ledger/submissions.csv, then refit the model with it. Biggest untapped data: 1 m 3DEP / GeoDAWN EarthMRI lidar (H4), which this sandbox cannot download.
