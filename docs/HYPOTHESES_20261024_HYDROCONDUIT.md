## Forensic Findings Motivating These 5 Untried Hypotheses (Verified Line by Line)

Before formulating new hypotheses, two previously undocumented empirical irregularities in the pinned feature stack and prior off-skeleton lanes were verified directly from disk:

1. **Band 6 (`tc`) Metadata Mislabelling Discovery:** In `training_features.tif`, Band 6 carries description `"tc - Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative for edge detection"`, yet across all 5,164,300 valid footprint pixels its values are strictly positive (`p05 = 11.8`, `p50 = 18.5`, `p95 = 25.6`) and correlate at **`r = 0.99714` with USGS GeoDAWN Airborne Gamma-Ray Spectrometry Total Count (`RAD TC` in `geodawn_rad_u8.tif`)**. It is **radiometric total count**, not magnetic tilt angle! Consequently, **no lane in `GEMSDOE92` has ever computed the true Reduced-to-Pole (`Band 2 rtp`) magnetic tilt/phase edge or hydrothermal magnetite-destruction trough.**
2. **The Range-Core Cliff Hijack (74.8%) & Scan-Order Keep Trap:** Unsupervised inspection of `BREAKLINE f45Ltop` (`||grad Band19|| * coherence(Band12)`) revealed that **74.8% of its 16,944 off-skeleton dots landed in the top detrended-elevation tercile (`det_elev >= +38.8 m`, bare mountain range cores)** and **28.9% aligned along E–W/WNW transverse erosional canyon orientations (`65°–135°` azimuth)**, leaving only **18.2% in the piedmont**. Because raw $\|\nabla \text{slope}\|$ scales with background slope (`25°–40°` cliffs vs `1.5°–5°` alluvial fans), un-normalized slope-break detectors miss subtle Quaternary piedmont scarps — where active Basin-and-Range normal faults and >75% of INGENIOUS blind geothermal systems actually localize (Wallace, 1977; Faulds et al., 2015, 2021). Meanwhile, restricting the skeleton keep stage to `R0`'s scan-order subset (`cand_mask = r0.copy()`) preserved `R0`'s range-core waste and `B2` deletion gaps.
3. **Overlooked Blind Chemical Geothermometry in GDR #1391 (`gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv`):** Prior `SPRINGALIGN` filtered solely on surface `temp_c >= 40 °C` (714 points), overlooking **419 unique blind geothermal well/spring pixels (732 records)** where shallow cold-aquifer mixing keeps surface `temp_c < 40 °C` while dissolved silica (`geothermquartz_c`, `geothermchalc_c`) and Na-K-Ca (`geothermcat_c`) chemical geothermometers record **`80–269 °C` deep reservoir temperatures**, as well as 121 rows tagged `'Hot '` with a trailing space.

## Pre-Registered Experimental Protocol for Rank 1 (`HYDROCONDUIT`)

### Named Non-Fault Process That Could Mimic `HYDROCONDUIT`
Piedmont alluvial-fan head/toe coalescing aprons (bajada break-in-slope transitions), non-tectonic Lake Lahontan recessional wave-cut shorelines sitting at piedmont elevations, and altered Tertiary volcanic flow margins or paleochannel aquifers carrying laterally outflowing thermal groundwater away from the true upflow fault.

### Three Pre-Registered Experiments (Budget Cap = 3)
All three experiments hold total emitted mass strictly at **37,654 unit-mass dots** (`1.0` inside footprint, `0.0` outside in primary `-zeros.tif`, `0` on known catalogue, `d_cat > 2.0 px`), differing **only** in the single controlled ablation (hydrothermal-RTP conduit multiplier off vs on) and single controlled off-skeleton dose step (`45%` vs `50%`):

1. **Experiment 1 (`f45pied` — Geomorphic-Tectonic Piedmont Conduit Baseline, 45% far):**
   - Surface: $S_{\text{pied}} = T_{\text{scarp}} \times A_{\text{tect}}$ (relative log-slope break $\times$ 600 m cross-strike monotonicity $\times$ elevation coherence $\times$ piedmont-elevation & shallow-basement hinge $\times$ Andersonian strike & strain compatibility).
   - Skeleton stage (`n_keep = 20,710`, `55.0%`): exact marginal expected-DTI (`select_exact_marginal_dti_add`) over legal `H19-5 & (d_cat > 2.0)` weighted by piedmont-tectonic habitat weight.
   - Off-skeleton stage (`n_far = 16,944`, `45.0%`): `spaced_top_mask(S_pied, far_pool, 16944, min_dist_px=2.8)` over `d_H19 > 3.0 px` and `d_cat > 2.0 px`.
2. **Experiment 2 (`f45hydro` — Full Hydrothermal-RTP Piedmont Conduit, 45% far):**
   - Surface: $S_{\text{hydro}} = S_{\text{pied}} \times (0.50 + 0.50\,G_{\text{hydro}})$, adding the multi-physics hydrothermal conduit factor (`Band 2 RTP` tilt-contact + positive-Laplacian magnetite-destruction trough, `GeoDAWN K/(Th+1)` potassic alteration, `Band 17` MT conductivity, and `GDR #1391` thermal + blind chemical geothermometer plume).
   - Identical `55.0% / 45.0%` (`20,710 / 16,944`) split — clean paired ablation of $G_{\text{hydro}}$ against `f45pied`.
3. **Experiment 3 (`f50hydro` — Full Hydrothermal-RTP Piedmont Conduit, 50% far):**
   - Same surface $S_{\text{hydro}}$, shifting budget to `50.0% / 50.0%` (`18,827` skeleton + `18,827` off-skeleton `d_H19 > 3.0 px`), capping maximum possible skeleton-lineage overlap at `<= 50.0%` by construction.
