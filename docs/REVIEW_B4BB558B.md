## Shared fixes and limits

- Shared registry summaries now reject empty/incomplete scans and malformed/truthy completeness values, derive stop flags from measured overlap and **absolute** rho, require SHA-256 pins, check original numeric ranges before float32 conversion, preserve the registry's original precision for ranks, reject missing in-footprint values, and verify a disk candidate's grid. Exact full-footprint rank attestation is required for a complete summary; sampled or unmarked historical rows cannot establish clearance. Row-only diagnostics stop pending an inventory certificate. Merging incremental audits requires matching valid candidate hashes, strict completeness and consistent thresholds.
- The shared writer verifies that the supplied footprint is the sample's actual finite mask and validates the input sample before output. It uses create-only hardlinks to avoid ordinary overwrite. Its rollback holds staged links to avoid inode-reuse errors, but check-then-unlink rollback is **not atomic** against noncooperating concurrent writers: independent writers must use distinct names. The format writer alone does not authorize uniqueness or submission.
- Additional legacy runners and finalizers whose scans followed work/placement now call the common unconditional stop first. This covers VARIO stages, current/recent named lanes and the directly tested finalizers, not every historical builder or every internal function. Treat remaining old builders as unaudited research code, not authorized entrypoints.
- The public pages now put the negative decision ahead of archives, label the old TIFF/ZIP as research-only, and link this evidence-bound run card and input receipt. Local checks establish a single-band float32 matching grid and finite `[0,1]` values, directly addressing the reported portal message **“Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”**. They do not prove organizer acceptance: the published instructions describe null/NaN outside the study-area bounds, whereas local files use zeros outside the sample finite footprint; equivalence and actual portal behavior remain unverified.
