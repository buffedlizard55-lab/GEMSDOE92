### Hypothesis H1 (`LISTRIC-RADIO-LOQO` — Primary Lane, Directly Executing `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md` Priorities #1 and #2)

1. **Specific Layers Used:**
   - **Subsurface Potential-Field & Structural Depth Stack (`training_features.tif`):**
     - Band 15 (`depth_to_base_surf`): Cenozoic basin-fill thickness $B(\mathbf{x})$ (m) from USGS gravity-basement inversion (Jachens & Moring 1990).
     - Band 5 (`iso_grav_anom_slope`), Band 13 (`iso_grav_anom`), Band 18 (`iso_grav_anom_hg`), Band 11 (`iso_grav_anom_vg`): Isostatic residual gravity anomaly $g_{\text{iso}}$, horizontal gradient $\|\nabla_H g_{\text{iso}}\|$, and first vertical derivative $\partial g_{\text{iso}}/\partial z$.
     - Band 2 (`rtp`), Band 3 (`tmi_hg`): Reduced-to-pole total magnetic intensity and horizontal gradient.
     - Band 17 (`cond_surf`): Magnetotelluric surface/shallow electrical conductivity $\sigma_{\text{MT}}$.
   - **Geomorphic & Geodetic Strain Stack (`training_features.tif`):**
     - Band 12 (`det_elev`), Band 19 (`det_elev_slope`): Regional detrended elevation $z_{\text{det}}$ and local topographic slope angle.
     - Band 4 (`geod_2ndinv`), Band 7 (`geod_shearrate`), Band 8 (`geod_dilaterate`): Geodetic second invariant, shear strain rate, and dilatation rate.
   - **Verified Official External Layers (`registry/session_manifest.json`, zero catalogue leakage):**

2. **Physical Signature Targeted:**
   - **Grauch-Cordell Updip Listric Projection (Grauch & Cordell 1987, *Geophysics* 52(1):118–131; Jachens & Moring 1990):**
     Normal faults in the Great Basin dip $\theta_{\text{dip}} \approx 45^\circ\text{–}60^\circ$ toward the hanging-wall basin. Because the density contrast ($\Delta \rho \approx -0.35\text{ to }-0.55\text{ g/cm}^3$) between pre-Cenozoic bedrock and Cenozoic basin fill integrates along the dipping fault ramp, the maximum horizontal gradient of isostatic gravity (`Band 5`, `Band 18`) and depth-to-basement (`Band 15`) is displaced **down-dip into the hanging wall** by $\Delta x_{\text{down-dip}} \approx z \cot \theta_{\text{dip}} \approx 200\text{–}500\text{ m}$ ($2.0\text{–}5.0\text{ px}$ at $100\text{ m/px}$).
     We construct the regional updip footwall-directed unit normal vector $\hat{\mathbf{n}}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x}) = (n_y, n_x)$ pointing from deep, low-gravity basin fill toward shallow, high-gravity, elevated footwall bedrock:
     $$\mathbf{v}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x}) = 0.45\,\frac{-\nabla \tilde{b}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})}{\|\nabla \tilde{b}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})\| + \varepsilon} + 0.35\,\frac{+\nabla \tilde{g}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})}{\|\nabla \tilde{g}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})\| + \varepsilon} + 0.20\,\frac{+\nabla \tilde{z}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})}{\|\nabla \tilde{z}_{3.0}(\mathbf{x})\| + \varepsilon}, \qquad \hat{\mathbf{n}}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{\mathbf{v}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x})}{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x})\| + \varepsilon}$$
     Looking **down-dip into the hanging wall** from surface candidate pixel $\mathbf{x}$ by offset $s > 0$ pixels samples $\mathbf{x}_{\text{down-dip}}(s) = \mathbf{x} - s\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}_{\text{updip}}(\mathbf{x})$. Evaluating the subsurface potential-field fault-ramp strength $L_{\text{sub}}$ at depth-scheduled listric offsets $\Delta_{\text{px}}(\mathbf{x}) = \operatorname{clip}(2.0 + 0.0045\,B_{2.5}(\mathbf{x}),\; 2.2,\; 4.6\text{ px})$ and multi-angle sheet offsets $s \in \{2.2, 3.4, 4.6\}\text{ px}$ projects the hanging-wall gravity/basement/MT ramp **updip onto the actual surface scarp, piedmont basal toe, incised canyon knickzone, or shallow bedrock subcrop shoulder**.
   - **Dual-Ratio Radioelement (`K/Th` + `U/Th`) Redox-Contact & Strike Concordance (Dickson & Scott 1977; Nash 2010):**
     Ascending geothermal/epithermal fluids produce potassic adularia-sericite alteration (elevated $K/\text{Th}$) and mobilize hexavalent uranium ($\text{U}^{6+}$) from felsic volcanic tuffs to precipitate tetravalent $\text{U}^{4+}$ at fault-zone redox barriers (elevated $U/\text{Th}$ and $\|\nabla(U/(\text{Th}+10))\|$), while fault-juxtaposed bedrock units create linear gradients in $(K/\text{Th}, U/\text{Th})$ whose strike angle $\theta_{\text{rad}}$ is concordant ($\cos^2(\theta_{\text{rad}} - \theta_{\text{sub}})$) with the updip-projected gravity/basement fault strike.
   - **Named Non-Fault Geological Processes That Could Mimic This Signature (Protocol Rule 5 Falsification):**
     1. *Buried paleo-valleys, caldera ring-fracture moat fills, and differential-compaction hinges* over pre-Tertiary erosional bedrock paleotopography can generate gravity/basement gradients updip of basin margins without Quaternary brittle slip; mitigated by requiring axial strike concordance, incised drainage-knickpoint curvature, or Mohr-Coulomb / radioelement contact alignment.
     2. *Felsic ash-flow tuff / rhyolite dome contacts and uranium-bearing tuffaceous lacustrine beds* can produce high `K/Th` and `U/Th` gradients; mitigated by coupling radioelement contact gradients with updip-projected basement/gravity steps ($\cos^2(\theta_{\text{rad}} - \theta_{\text{sub}})$) and suppressing flat-lying basin-floor interiors.

3. **Why It Catches Faults Missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS Catalogue (`existing_faults.tif`):**
   - **Pediment-veneered & eroded intrabasin faults:** Aerial-photo and Qfaults mapping heavily favors fresh, steep range-front scarps ($\text{slope} > 6^\circ$). Faults concealed beneath thin alluvial fans or degraded into gentle $1^\circ\text{–}5^\circ$ pediment steps and incised wash knickpoints are systematically omitted from `existing_faults.tif`, yet preserve a sharp dipping Bouguer/basement step $200\text{–}450\text{ m}$ down-dip in the hanging wall plus a `K/Th`–`U/Th` contact boundary at the subcrop.
   - **`NE` Carbonate Terrane bedrock faults (`41.6%` of unmapped `USGS SGMC` faults):** As established in `#IR-92-NE-QUADRANT-DOUBLE-EDGED-CALIBRATION`, the `NE` quadrant has median `geod_shearrate = 0.00` and low volcanic magnetic gradient (`tmi_hg = 6.4`), but the highest isostatic gravity slope (`0.129`) and intense carbonate/siliciclastic radioelement contrasts.

4. **How It Differs from Existing Implementations in `GEMSDOE92`:**
   - `KNICKLOQO` (`gems/knick_loqo.py`), `POISSONKNICK` (`gems/poissonknick.py`), `POISSONFLEX` (`gems/poissonflex.py`), and `PROVINCETOE` (`gems/provincetoe.py`) evaluate subsurface gravity/basement ridges (`L_sub`) **in situ at pixel $\mathbf{x}$ ($s = 0$)**, leaving subsurface dots $2\text{–}5\text{ px}$ down-dip in the hanging wall where the $300\text{ m}$ ($3.0\text{ px}$) triangular evaluation kernel on `d_visible > 2.0` has decayed or vanished.
   - `KNICKLOQO` ignored `Band 3 (U)` of `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` completely, whereas `LISTRICLOQO` incorporates both `U/Th` redox-front mobilization (`f25_rad_uth_redox_grad`) and directional strike concordance between the $(K/\text{Th}, U/\text{Th})$ contact tensor and the updip-projected basement/gravity ramp (`f26_rad_grav_concord`), plus relay-ramp longitudinal basement transfer (`f27_relay_strike_transfer`, Hypothesis H2) and playa-filtered MT conduit gradients (`f28_mt_fault_conduit`, Hypothesis H3).
   - Geometrically, projecting the subsurface ridge branch updip by $2.2\text{–}4.6\text{ px}$ moves subsurface candidate dots out of the $3.0\text{ px}$ disks of `KNICKLOQO`'s in-situ down-dip `L_sub` dots while moving them **closer** to the surface/subcrop fault trace.
