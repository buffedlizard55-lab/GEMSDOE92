## 3. Mathematical Specification of Top Candidate: `RIEDELTENSOR` (`gems/riedel_tensor.py`)

### 3.1 Four New Catalogue-Free Physical Operators (36 Features Total)

1. **Laplace-Consistent 3D Gravity-Gradient Tensor Tilt & Step-Dipole Concordance ($\Theta_{gz}, \mathcal{D}_{gz}$, Pedersen & Rasmussen 1990; Beiki 2010):**
   Let $g = G_{2.0}(\text{Band 13})$ be smoothed isostatic gravity anomaly and $g_z = G_{1.5}(\text{Band 11})$ be vertical gravity gradient $\partial_z g$.
   - The horizontal-vertical tensor components are $G_{xz} = \partial_x g_z$ and $G_{yz} = \partial_y g_z$, with horizontal magnitude $\|\mathbf{g}_{hz}\|_2 = \sqrt{G_{xz}^2 + G_{yz}^2}$.
   - By Laplace's equation ($\partial_{xx} g + \partial_{yy} g + \partial_{zz} g = 0$ in source-free space), the vertical-vertical tensor component is $G_{zz} = -(\partial_{xx} g + \partial_{yy} g) = -\nabla_h^2 g$.
   - The **3D Gravity-Tensor Vertical-Gradient Tilt** is:
     $$\Theta_{gz}(x) = \frac{\|\mathbf{g}_{hz}(x)\|_2}{\sqrt{\|\mathbf{g}_{hz}(x)\|_2^2 + G_{zz}(x)^2 + \epsilon_g^2}} \in [0, 1], \qquad \epsilon_g = 0.015$$
     which peaks directly over steep basement fault contacts with $1/z^3$ depth localization and is invariant to province-scale density contrast amplitude!
   - The **Signed Gravity Step-Dipole Concordance** is:
     $$\mathcal{D}_{gz}(x) = -\frac{\nabla_h g(x) \cdot \mathbf{g}_{hz}(x)}{\|\nabla_h g(x)\|_2 \, \|\mathbf{g}_{hz}(x)\|_2 + \epsilon}$$
     which is positive along normal-fault footwall-to-hanging-wall density steps where $g_z$ transitions from positive (footwall) to negative (hanging wall) in the direction of $-\nabla_h g$.

2. **En-Echelon Synthetic Riedel ($R, R'$) Transtensional Relay Obliquity ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{Riedel}}$, Faulds & Hinz 2015):**
   Let $\theta_{\text{topo}}(x) \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ)$ be the dominant topographic structure-tensor strike (`Band 12`) with coherence $C_{\text{topo}}(x)$, and let $\theta_{\text{deep}}(x) \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ)$ be the composite potential-field (gravity `Band 13` + RTP magnetic `Band 2`) structure-tensor strike with coherence $C_{\text{deep}}(x) = \max(C_{\text{grav}}(x), C_{\text{RTP}}(x))$.
   - The acute axial strike divergence is:
     $$\Delta\theta(x) = \frac{180^\circ}{\pi} \arcsin\Bigl(\bigl|\sin\bigl(\theta_{\text{topo}}(x) - \theta_{\text{deep}}(x)\bigr)\bigr|\Bigr) \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ].$$
   - The **Synthetic Riedel ($R$-Shear) Transtensional Relay Index** targets the Coulomb-predicted Riedel obliquity $\theta_R = 15^\circ + \phi/2 \approx 19^\circ$ ($\sigma_R = 11^\circ$):
     $$\mathcal{R}_{\text{Riedel}}(x) = \exp\!\left(-\frac{(\Delta\theta(x) - 19^\circ)^2}{2\,(11^\circ)^2}\right) \cdot \sqrt{C_{\text{topo}}(x)\,C_{\text{deep}}(x)}.$$
   - Coupled with 4.5 km mesoscale-standardized shear/dilation strain $Z_{\text{Coulomb}}(x)$, $\mathcal{R}_{\text{Riedel}}(x) \cdot Z_{\text{Coulomb}}(x)$ isolates active transtensional step-overs, relay ramps, and horse-tail fault tips.

3. **3D Radiometric-Potential Alteration Contact Coupling ($\mathcal{A}_{\text{rad-pot}}$):**
   Let $\|\nabla_h(K/\text{Th})\|_2$ and $\|\nabla_h(U/\text{Th})\|_2$ be the horizontal gradients of potassium-over-thorium and uranium-over-thorium ratios from `ext_geodawn_rad_u8.tif`, and let $M_{\text{MT}}(x) = \|\nabla_h\log(\max(\text{Band 17}, 0.1))\|_2$ be the MT conductive clay-cap margin gradient.
   - The **Radiometric-Potential Alteration Contact Index** is:
     $$\mathcal{A}_{\text{rad-pot}}(x) = \Bigl(0.55\,\text{sat}\bigl(\|\nabla_h(K/\text{Th})\|_2, 0.08\bigr) + 0.45\,\text{sat}\bigl(\|\nabla_h(U/\text{Th})\|_2, 0.05\bigr)\Bigr) \cdot \max\bigl(\Theta_{gz}(x),\, \text{Tilt}_{\text{RTP}}(x)\bigr) \cdot \bigl(0.60 + 0.40\,\text{sat}(M_{\text{MT}}(x), 0.012)\bigr)$$
     which distinguishes fault-hosted hydrothermal alteration contacts (where K/U radiometric edges coincide with basement gravity-tensor / RTP magnetic contacts and MT clay-cap margins) from unmineralized surficial volcanic ash-flow tuff contacts.

4. **Wallace Basal Piedmont Toe Concavity + Selective Province-Debiased Calibration (`#IR-92-NE-QUADRANT-DOUBLE-EDGED-CALIBRATION` Cure):**
   - Incorporates the **Wallace Basal Slope-Toe Concavity** $\mathcal{B}_{\text{toe}}(x) = \lambda_+(\nabla^2 G_{1.5}(z)) \cdot \mu_{\text{step}}(x) \cdot \mathcal{A}_{\text{apron}}(x)$ (upward-concave slope break at the base of range fronts where downhill apron slope is gentler than uphill scarp face, suppressing symmetric V-canyons and summit ridges) alongside incised thalweg knickzones $\mathcal{K}_{\text{thalweg}}(x)$.
