## 3. Detailed Physical Justification, Catalogue-Gap Mechanism, Non-Fault Mimics & Repo Novelty Audit

### `H-92A` (`RIESZ-PIEDMONT`): Multi-Octave 2D Riesz Monogenic Step-Phase Congruency $\times$ Deconfounded 10 m Piedmont Scarp

Because the Riesz odd-phase congruency $\text{PC}_{\text{step}}(\mathbf{x})$ normalizes the odd Hilbert-Riesz vector magnitude $\|R u_\lambda\|$ by instantaneous monogenic amplitude $A_\lambda = \sqrt{u_\lambda^2 + \|R u_\lambda\|^2}$ and subtracts even-symmetry ridge/trough energy $|u_\lambda|$, a `1 km`-deep buried step offset attains the same phase congruency ($\varphi = \pm\pi/2 \Rightarrow \text{PC}_{\text{step}} \to 1$) as a shallow bedrock fault.
- **Named non-fault mimic process:** (1) **Symmetric Tertiary volcanic feeder dykes, basaltic cinder-cone chains, and buried pluton cupolas** (even symmetry $\varphi \in \{0, \pi\}$, where $|u_\lambda| > \|R u_\lambda\| \Rightarrow \text{PC}_{\text{step}} = 0$); and (2) **steep mountain-interior bedrock canyon walls and talus aprons** (suppressed by regressing out `100 m` `det_elev_slope` and gating down high positive `det_elev` range cores).
- **Difference from all 45 modules in `gems/`:** Verified via repository-wide AST/grep audit (`0` occurrences of `riesz`, `monogenic`, `hilbert`, or `congruency` in `gems/*.py`). Prior edge modules (`anasig.py`, `gravquad.py`, `tiltangle.py`, `thermotilt.py`, `eulerfuse.py`) used spatial Gaussian/FFT derivatives or `atan2(vg, hg)` without odd-vs-even monogenic symmetry cancellation.
