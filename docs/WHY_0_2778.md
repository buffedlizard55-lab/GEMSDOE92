# GEMSDOE32's reported `0.2778`: a bounded, evidence-led explanation

**Status: `0.2778` is a `CLAIM`, not an `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` score.** No submission receipt is stored in this repository. The user-referenced `0.3195` and `0.4413` are also unverified here and conflict with one another as descriptions of the higher result. This note explains a plausible metric-aligned mechanism; it does not reconstruct hidden labels, certify the score, or establish causation.

## Executive finding

The strongest defensible explanation is geometric: the R0 raster is a sparse, binary thinning of an older H19-5 linework pattern, with a clearance around the mapped-fault catalogue. Historical geometry receipts show that thinning reduced the number of emitted pixels and neighbour redundancy until spacing approached the official 300 m distance kernel; the catalogue collar then removed additional predictions near mapped faults. Under a distance-tolerant metric, fewer well-spaced predictions can improve the precision/recall trade-off even when some line coverage is lost.

This explains **why that construction could plausibly score well**. It does not show that every retained dot is a real fault, that the reported score is accurate, that the collar contains no hidden faults, or that the construction is globally optimal.

## Metric mechanism

The local reference implementation follows the repository's transcription of the competition metric. With hidden fault pixels `G`, prediction `p(x)`, known-fault mask `F`, valid footprint `Ω`, and triangular kernel `k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)`, it computes:

\[
TP_w=\sum_{g\in G}\max_{x\in\Omega\setminus F}p(x)k(d(x,g)),\quad
FP_w=\sum_{x\in\Omega\setminus F}p(x)\left[1-\max_{g\in G}k(d(x,g))\right],\quad
FN_w=|G|-TP_w,
\]

\[
DTI=\frac{TP_w}{TP_w+0.2FP_w+0.8FN_w}.
\]

The max operators matter. Several nearby dots can provide little extra `TP_w` once the same truth cells already have high cover, while each emitted dot still contributes to prediction mass and may add false-positive weight. Thinning a linework raster can therefore improve DTI if the reduction in redundant/off-target mass outweighs the lost coverage. A distance collar around mapped faults can similarly remove mass in regions that the organizer masks or discounts, but the score alone cannot establish what hidden labels occur there.

## Why R0's construction is plausible

The historical forensic receipt records this owner-reported score sequence and associated raster counts:

| Stage | Positive pixels (`GEOMETRY`, historical receipt) | Reported value |
|---|---:|---:|
| H19-5 skeleton | 121,131 | `CLAIM 0.1922` |
| D1.5 thinning | 60,069 | `CLAIM 0.2477` |
| D2.8 thinning | 44,090 | `CLAIM 0.2600` |
| D2.8 plus `d_cat > 1 px` | 40,199 | `CLAIM 0.2708` |
| R0 plus `d_cat > 2 px` | 37,654 | `CLAIM 0.2778` |

These are **not** a controlled organizer-side ablation. The similarity between geometry changes and reported score changes is suggestive, not proof. The geometry receipt also records R0 median nearest-neighbour spacing of 3 px and zero dots at catalogue distance `≤2 px`; those properties are consistent with the 3-pixel kernel and a conservative catalogue buffer. They say nothing by themselves about hidden-fault recall.

## Claims explicitly not made

- **Not “globally optimal.”** The metric is nonlinear in the full prediction field; a local marginal argument cannot establish a global optimum across all rasters or point budgets.
- **Not a “proof” of exact lineage in this checkout.** A historical JSON receipt records an exact mask comparison, but the required pinned inputs are now absent and the recipe is not retained. The attempted current rerun stopped before analysis.
- **Not proof that no hidden faults lie within 100–200 m of mapped faults.** Similar score increments per removed pixel do not identify the withheld truth or establish a causal false-positive-only effect.
- **Not an organizer-confirmed leaderboard score.** No receipt was found for `0.2778`, `0.3195`, or `0.4413`. The latter two conflict in the supplied context and cannot be ranked from this evidence.
- **Not a validated leaderboard forecast.** A fitted `MODEL` value is not a score; the estimator audit disclaims valid leaderboard prediction and individual intervals.

## Practical implication

R0 is a useful **historical geometry baseline**, not an approved artifact to resubmit. Its raster is pinned, but the current feature cache is incomplete, its exact construction cannot be reproduced from the present checkout, and the historical Focus-9 scan records non-uniqueness. The current TIPCOMP and RIESZ candidates are both machine-negative and **not eligible for submission**. See the [current follow-up review](REVIEW_20261028_9649DFC1.md), the [Focus-9 comparison](focus9.html), and [submission instructions](how-to-submit.html).

A responsible attempt to improve on the reported value first needs (1) the missing assigned method paragraph and an agreed uniqueness interpretation; (2) authenticated, rights-cleared inputs and the complete pinned feature cache; and (3) a newly authorized lane evaluated with the shared spatial holdout, per-feature leakage canaries, pre-placement and final-dot registry checks, and the common submission writer. Until then, no numerical improvement projection or new TIFF is justified.
