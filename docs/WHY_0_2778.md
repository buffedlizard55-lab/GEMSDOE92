## 1. Exact Tversky Kernel Algebra of the Official GEMS Metric

Per the [official problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and reference notebook (`drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution`), let $G \subset \Omega \setminus F_{\text{known}}$ be the hidden new-fault pixel set inside the `5,167,373`-cell valid footprint $\Omega$ with mapped USGS/INGENIOUS faults $F_{\text{known}}$ masked pixel-exactly ([DrivenData staff thread 11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)). With the 300 m (`3 px`) radial triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(0, 1 - d/3)$:

$$TP_w = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{x \in \Omega \setminus F_{\text{known}}} p(x)\,k\!\bigl(\|x - g\|_2\bigr), \qquad FP_w = \sum_{x \in \Omega \setminus F_{\text{known}}} p(x)\,\Bigl[1 - \max_{g \in G} k\!\bigl(\|x - g\|_2\bigr)\Bigr], \qquad FN_w = |G| - TP_w$$

$$\mathrm{DTI}(p) = \frac{TP_w}{TP_w + 0.2\,FP_w + 0.8\,FN_w} = \frac{TP_w}{0.2\,TP_w + 0.2\,FP_w + 0.8\,|G|}.$$

Three exact mathematical properties govern every leaderboard score in the 8-owner GEMSDOE fleet:

1. **Bang-Bang (`{0, 1}`) Optimality.** For a single isolated pixel $x$, both marginal truth credit $\Delta TP_w(x)$ and marginal false-positive penalty $\Delta FP_w(x)$ are homogeneous of degree 1 in $p(x) \in [0, 1]$. Consequently $\partial\,\mathrm{DTI}/\partial p(x)$ has constant sign on $[0, 1]$, making corners $p(x) \in \{0.0, 1.0\}$ globally optimal. Intermediate continuous probabilities (`GEMSDOE36` `b1-ens3 = 0.1647`, `GEMSDOE28` `ens-raw-mean = 0.0206`) dilute $TP_w$ across millions of background cells while accumulating huge $0.2\,FP_w$ denominator mass.
2. **Asymmetric Recall Dominance ($\beta/\alpha = 4.0$).** Adding one non-overlapping unit dot ($p(x)=1$) with expected 3-px kernel coverage $\Delta T = \mathbb{E}[\Delta TP_w(x)]$ and self-proximity credit $c_0 = \mathbb{E}[\max_g k(\|x-g\|)]$ increases $\mathrm{DTI}$ iff
   $$\Delta T \cdot (1 - 0.2\,\mathrm{DTI}) > 0.2\,\mathrm{DTI}\cdot (1 - c_0).$$
   At $\mathrm{DTI} = 0.2778$, the break-even threshold is $\Delta T > 0.0588\,(1 - c_0)$: **any pixel whose 300 m triangular kernel covers at least `0.059` expected hidden-fault pixels (~2% per-pixel fault probability over the 29-cell disk) strictly improves leaderboard DTI**.
3. **1D Line Thinning Law ($1 - s/12$).** Along a 1D fault trace where truth coincides with the predicted skeleton, unit dots spaced every $s \in (0, 6)$ pixels achieve mean kernel recall
   $$\bar{k}(s) = \frac{2}{s}\int_0^{s/2}\!\left(1 - \frac{u}{3}\right)du = 1 - \frac{s}{12},$$
   while cutting $FP_w$ by factor $1/s$. At $s = 2.8\text{ px}$ (`280 m`), a skeleton retains **`76.7%` of full continuous-line recall (`1 - 2.8/12 = 0.7667`)** while slashing false positives by **`2.8x`**!
