## Executive finding

The strongest defensible explanation is geometric: the R0 raster is a sparse, binary thinning of an older H19-5 linework pattern, with a clearance around the mapped-fault catalogue. Historical geometry receipts show that thinning reduced the number of emitted pixels and neighbour redundancy until spacing approached the official 300 m distance kernel; the catalogue collar then removed additional predictions near mapped faults. Under a distance-tolerant metric, fewer well-spaced predictions can improve the precision/recall trade-off even when some line coverage is lost.

This explains **why that construction could plausibly score well**. It does not show that every retained dot is a real fault, that the reported score is accurate, that the collar contains no hidden faults, or that the construction is globally optimal.

## Metric mechanism

The local reference implementation follows the repository's transcription of the competition metric. With hidden fault pixels `G`, prediction `p(x)`, known-fault mask `F`, valid footprint `Ω`, and triangular kernel `k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)`, it computes:

\[
TP_w=\sum_{g\in G}\max_{x\in\Omega\setminus F}p(x)k(d(x,g)),\quad
FP_w=\sum_{x\in\Omega\setminus F}p(x)\left[1-\max_{g\in G}k(d(x,g))\right],\quad
FN_w=|G|-TP_w,
\]

\[
DTI=\frac{TP_w}{TP_w+0.2FP_w+0.8FN_w}.
\]

The max operators matter. Several nearby dots can provide little extra `TP_w` once the same truth cells already have high cover, while each emitted dot still contributes to prediction mass and may add false-positive weight. Thinning a linework raster can therefore improve DTI if the reduction in redundant/off-target mass outweighs the lost coverage. A distance collar around mapped faults can similarly remove mass in regions that the organizer masks or discounts, but the score alone cannot establish what hidden labels occur there.

### 1. Why `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` (`GEMSDOE32`) reached `0.2778` (`CLAIM`)

From a structural-geology and potential-field standpoint, `R0` (`37,654` binary `1.0` dots, `c55bafc47005…`) succeeded across the Focus-9 cohort (`GEMSDOE24` `0.2477`, `GEMSDOE25` `0.2600`, `GEMSDOE27` `0.2690`, `GEMSDOE28` `0.2708`, `GEMSDOE30` `0.2708`, `GEMSDOE31` `0.2768`, `GEMSDOE32` `0.2778`, `GEMSDOE33` `0.2778`, `GEMSDOE36` `0.1647` — all owner `CLAIM`s, not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` receipts) because three physical and metric effects reinforce one another:

1. **Multi-channel Basin-and-Range range-front ridge fusion (`H19-5`):** In northwestern Great Basin extensional geothermal systems (Dixie Valley, Granite Springs, Bradys–Desert Peak, Humboldt House), permeable range-bounding normal faults juxtapose dense, magnetized Mesozoic/Cenozoic footwall blocks ($\rho \approx 2.65\text{–}2.75\text{ g cm}^{-3}$) against unconsolidated Neogene–Quaternary basin fill ($\rho \approx 2.0\text{–}2.2\text{ g cm}^{-3}$, `B15 depth_to_base_surf`). The resulting horizontal gravity gradient (`B18 iso_grav_anom_hg` / `B13 iso_grav_anom`), RTP magnetic contact gradient (`B02 rtp` / `B03 tmi_hg`), and 3DEP detrended break-in-slope relief (`B12 det_elev` / `B19 det_elev_slope`) co-localize along unmapped piedmont splay faults and concealed basinward step-faults.
2. **Kernel-matched Poisson-disk packing (`d = 2.8 px = 280 m`) under $\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8$:** Because $TP_w = \sum_{g \in G} \max_x p(x) k(d(x, g))$ uses a **pointwise maximum** over the `300 m` (`3 px`) triangular kernel while $FP_w = \sum_x p(x)[1 - \max_g k(d(x, g))]$ **sums linearly** over every emitted pixel, dense `1 px` skeletons (`121,131` px, `CLAIM 0.1922`) pay $3\times$ the false-positive penalty along every barren segment while adding minimal incremental $TP_w$ along true segments. Thinning to `2.8 px` (`280 m < 300 m`) preserves continuous triangular-kernel coverage along linear fault strikes while cutting off-fault false positives by `~69%`. Note also that emitting fractional values $p \in (0, 1)$ scales linear $FP_w$ everywhere while scaling $TP_w$ down on isolated hits, which is why binary `{0.0, 1.0}` dot sets dominate soft probability rasters (`GEMSDOE36` `p97` `CLAIM 0.1647`) at fixed support.
3. **Catalogue-collar truncation (`d_cat > 2 px = 200 m`):** Mapped USGS/INGENIOUS faults (`F`, `60,988` px) are masked from evaluation, and any dot at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$ wastes `35–65%` of its `300 m` kernel disc inside masked catalogue cells or unmapped digitizing offsets of already-mapped traces rather than genuinely unmapped stepover/basinward structures.
