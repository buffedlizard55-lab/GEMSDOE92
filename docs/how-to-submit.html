How to submit when a candidate is cleared

  1. Download the exact cleared single-band GeoTIFF (or ZIP with one GeoTIFF), without re-saving.
  2. Open the official submission page, check the weekly limit displayed there, and upload the file.
  3. Use the chosen unique name and short note, submit, and record the organizer receipt. Do not use the historical name as a new submission.

For “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”, validate actual raw pixels for non-finite values, nodata sentinels and values outside [0,1], including exterior pixels. Confirm CRS, shape and transform against the official template. Local checks cannot guarantee portal acceptance.
