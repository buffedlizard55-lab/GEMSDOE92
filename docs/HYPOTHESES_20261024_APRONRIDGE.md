## 2. Named Non-Fault Process That Can Mimic Rank-1 (`APRONRIDGE`)

Even after `H_apron` suppresses deep central-basin Lake Lahontan stillstands ($d_{15} \gg 600\text{ m}, z_{12} \ll p_{33}$) and high bedrock cliffs ($d_{15} \to 0, z_{12} \gg p_{67}$), **three non-fault processes** inside the intermediate piedmont apron can still mimic `APRONRIDGE`:

1. **Intermediate-elevation pluvial shoreline benches and Gilbert-delta foreset toes** (e.g., Sehoo / Eetza recessional strandlines draped across piedmont fans at $100\text{–}300\text{ m}$ cover depth), which create linear slope-breaks along contours.
2. **Incised alluvial-fan terrace risers and entrenched ephemeral wash walls**, where stream entrenchment cuts linear scarps across piedmont aprons without tectonic slip.
3. **Remanently magnetized Tertiary basaltic-andesite flow margins or shallow sills** pinched out under thin piedmont gravel, producing sharp `Band 2 rtp` gradients and `Band 6 tc` curvature anomalies across non-tectonic paleotopographic edges.

## 3. Pre-Registered 3-Experiment Design & Decision Rule

All three budgeted experiments use the **single frozen `APRONRIDGE` surface `S_raw(x)`** (`gems/apronridge.py`), zero catalogue inputs in `S_raw`, exact unit-mass budget `N = 37,654` dots (`d_cat > 2.0 px`, zero catalogue mass), varying only the structural skeleton coupling fraction along a clean 3-point ablation:

1. **Experiment 1 (`s60f40`)**: `22,592` dots (`60.0%`) emitted across the **full eligible `H19-5` network** (`h19 & valid & d_cat > 2.0`, **not** restricted to `R0`'s scan-order subset) via exact marginal expected-DTI greedy (`scripts/exact_dti_refill.py`) under $w_{\text{skel}} = H_{\text{apron}} \cdot (0.25 + 0.75\,S_{\text{pct}})$ on `h19` (`0.10\,S_{\text{raw}}$ off `h19`, preserving physical dynamic range across cover/relief regimes rather than flattening it with a `0.30` uniform floor), plus `15,062` far dots (`40.0%`) emitted via `spaced_top_mask(S_raw, min_dist_px=2.8)` on 1D ridge crests strictly `> 3.0 px` off `H19-5`.
2. **Experiment 2 (`s50f50`)**: `18,827` dots (`50.0%`) on non-R0 `H19-5` via exact marginal expected-DTI greedy + `18,827` far dots (`50.0%`) on 1D `APRONRIDGE` crests `> 3.0 px` off `H19-5`.
3. **Experiment 3 (`free1d`)**: `37,654` dots (`100.0%` standalone) emitted directly on `valid & ridge_1d & (d_cat > 2.0)` via `spaced_top_mask(S_raw, min_dist_px=2.8)` — **zero use of `R0` or `H19-5`**.
