# Pre-Implementation Geological Hypothesis Shortlist — 2026-10-24 (`SCARPDIPOLE` Session)

## Scope & Protocol Discipline

Before building any raster in this session, five untried geological hypotheses were formulated against the SHA-256-pinned Basin-and-Range data stack (`.cache/gems_inputs/training_features.tif`, 19 bands; `.cache/gems_inputs/external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif`, 4 USGS GeoDAWN radiometric bands; `.cache/gems_inputs/external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv`, DOE GDR 1391 thermal wells/springs; `.cache/gems_inputs/derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif`, USGS SGMC bedrock-fault control).

Every hypothesis respects the empirical band-transform law verified in `GEMSDOE32`:
- **Never re-differentiate pre-differentiated bands** (`Band 6 tc` terrain correction, `Band 7 geod_shearrate` GPS shear rate, `Band 3 tmi_hg`), which amplifies grid/kriging ringing.
- **Differentiate only raw potential or topographic fields** (`Band 12 det_elev`, `Band 4 iso_grav_anom`, `Band 2 rtp`).
- **Zero catalogue pixels or labels** enter any feature transform or surface `S(y, x)`.

In `R0`, thinning from `121,131` `H19-5` pixels down to `37,654` dots discarded `83,477` `H19-5` pixels largely by arbitrary scan-order spacing rather than geomorphic scarp expression.
