Why 0.2778? What would it take to beat it?

Under the competition's pooled asymmetric Distance-Tolerant Tversky Index (α = 0.2 on false positives, β = 0.8 on false negatives, 300 m triangular Euclidean kernel; Official Rules §2), false negatives carry 4× the penalty weight of false positives, and evaluation is performed against newly expert-labeled faults derived from USGS GeoDAWN magnetics/radiometrics and 1 m 3DEP lidar while known USGS/INGENIOUS fault pixels are excluded. R0 (h33-h33-2-b2) descends from H19-5 → D2.8 (44,090 ridge/knickzone dots) followed by a >2 px (>200 m) catalogue-distance collar leaving 37,654 dots: removing the 6,436 near-catalogue pixels reallocates mass out of the 300 m kernel shadow of already-mapped faults without sacrificing regional basin-margin ridge coverage. By contrast, pixelwise Positive-Unlabeled gradient boosting in GEMSDOE28 s21-puhgb achieved the highest static catalogue-holdout DTI among the new October 26 artifacts (0.034629) yet dropped to 0.0206 CLAIM, consistent with overfitting mapped-fault flank habitat rather than continuous unmapped GeoDAWN/3DEP fault traces, whereas submodular ridge/lineament placement in GEMSDOE27 h31-drcg-wl-celf28 reached 0.2250 CLAIM despite 0.5023 overlap vs R0. That structural contrast is a physical-statistical explanation, not causal proof without organizer ground truth.
