Why 0.2778? What would it take to beat it?

R0 (h33-h33-2-b2) is a descendant of H19-5: D2.8 has 44,090 dots, then a >2-pixel catalogue-distance filter leaves 37,654. Thinning can reduce redundant predicted mass while retaining nearby credit under a 300 m triangular kernel. That is a plausible mechanism, not causal proof of the claimed value. DrivenData staff clarify that the known-fault mask is pixel-exact, not a surrounding buffer: nearby unmasked predictions are evaluated normally, and new labels can lie within 300 m. Removing a wider collar may therefore discard potentially useful predictions; any effect needs a controlled test.

Before a slot, a newly assigned method would need a genuine spatially blocked, fold-rebuilt, visible-fault-only holdout win over the same-evaluator best control, individual feature leakage canaries, complete pre-placement and final uniqueness checks, and byte-bound format validation. The five ranked hypotheses are untried; the top one is blocked, not disproven.
