## Candidate 1 result and interpretation

**HOLDOUT-DTI** (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`; 8 buffered spatial folds from seeds 20 and 30; 22,663 withheld positives; 1,000 paired 20 km spatial-cluster bootstrap draws): candidate `0.046866` (95% CI `[0.040216, 0.053315]`) versus R0 `0.004006` (`[0.003424, 0.004690]`); paired difference `+0.042860` (`[0.036122, 0.049357]`). Each fold's placement corridor was derived from its visible catalogue only; hidden catalogue segments were not used to construct the candidate. Leakage canary, visible-fault distance alone: symmetric AUC `0.745593`, below the `0.90` stop threshold.

The result is a large gain on **catalogue hide-and-recover**, not evidence that new uncatalogued faults have been found. It may exploit the designed relationship between held-out catalogue components and visible components. The live competition truth is private and differs in that key respect. No leaderboard score can be inferred from this local number.

The pre-placement surface and final dots were compared with the 14 locally accessible, hash-verified rasters in `evidence/lane_registry_index.json`: worst absolute Spearman rank correlation `0.012813` (surface) / `0.007341` (dots); worst forward 3-px overlap `0.007209` / `0.006958`. These measurements establish uniqueness only against that local index—not against every raster across the GEMSDOE sites listed in the operator prompt.
