## Pass 1 — independent facts before implementing anything geological

1. **What the prize actually evaluates.** The [DrivenData problem statement](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) asks for **fault** predictions; some existing mapped faults are incomplete or inaccurate, and hidden new faults are expert-labelled. It describes a second review round using expanded labels. A gravity anomaly, thermal spring, or vent is therefore **not** automatically a correct fault prediction or a verified geothermal reservoir. Official DTI has `k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)`, `alpha=0.2` on false-positive weight and `beta=0.8` on false-negative weight. At the template's 100 m cells, its positive kernel extends 3 pixels.
2. **Forensics on the owner's purported `0.2778`.** I independently reopened `data/bridge/{h19_5,d28,h27_4_solo,labels,sample_submission}.tif` and `data/parent_R0_zeros.tif` and measured exactly 121,131 / 44,090 / 40,199 / 37,654 positive footprint cells in that order. Set differences are zero for `R0 ⊂ SOLO ⊂ D28 ⊂ H19-5`. R0 is finite `[0,1]`, single-band float32, `EPSG:32611`, `3730×3292`, median nearest-neighbour spacing 3 px, **zero** dots on the 60,988 catalogue pixels, minimum catalogue distance `sqrt(5)` px, and 2,171 dots at distance ≤3 px. This verifies a *geometry*, not its private-label performance. Sparse, spaced, catalogue-avoiding emission is a defensible DTI mechanism: overlapping predictions may earn no extra truth credit yet still cost FP weight. But whether it caused any leaderboard gain **cannot be established** without the same test labels/receipt or a controlled scored ablation. [GEMSDOE32's own page](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html) calls its H33-2-B2 file **UNSCORED** and explicitly says *no organiser score exists*. Consequently `0.2778` is a **USER-REPORTED CLAIM**, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The owner's earlier `0.2708` is also not a receipt in this repo. The file's finite-zero encoding addresses the reported `[0,1]` form rejection; it does not demonstrate geologic improvement or organizer acceptance here.
