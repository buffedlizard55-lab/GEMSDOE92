Layers Involved
Bands 2 (rtp), 4 (geod_2ndinv), 5 (iso_grav_anom_slope), 7 (geod_shearrate), 8 (geod_dilaterate), 11 (iso_grav_anom_vg), 12 (det_elev), 15 (depth_to_base_surf), 17 (cond_surf), 19 (det_elev_slope) + geodawn_rad_u8.tif (K, Th, U) + gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv (≥80 °C blind geothermometers).
Physical Signature
Projects volumetric dilatation (Band 8) onto N18°E opening cos²(θ-18°) and shear √(B7·B4) onto ±45° Walker Lane / Humboldt conjugates sin²(2(θ-15°)) (both zero at N105°E clamping/wash azimuths), fusing piedmont monoclinal scarps, FFT upward-continued RTP (100/300/600 m) + gravity tilt + MT clay-cap lateral edges + radiometric contact gradients, and corroborated bedrock relays along bilinear Canny cross-strike 1D NMS centrelines.

Named Non-Fault Mimics
