1. APRONRIDGE — Thin-Cover Piedmont Accommodation Apron & Inclination-Corrected RTP-Curvature 1D Ridges

Layers Involved
Band 15 (depth_to_base_surf), Band 12 (det_elev), Band 5 (iso_grav_anom_slope), Band 19 (det_elev_slope), Band 2 (rtp, Reduced-to-Pole magnetics), and Band 6 (tc, total curvature) — Bands 2, 6, and 15 were unused by all 13 prior built lanes.
Physical Signature
S_raw = H_apron(B12, B15 ~20–600 m log-normal, B5) × max(R_scarp[B19,B12], 0.85 × R_rtp[||∇B2|| × (-B6) × coh(B2)]) restricted to 4-axis 1D directional crests (directional_ridge_1d).
Why Missing from Catalogue
Synthetic hanging-wall step-faults and shallow-buried basement transfer faults in the thin-cover piedmont apron either offset homogeneous fan gravels (zero gravity worm) or have degraded surface scarps where raw TMI is laterally skewed 300–800 m by ~65° geomagnetic inclination; RTP centers the magnetic gradient directly over the vertical basement step.
Named Non-Fault Mimics
Sehoo/Eetza pluvial shoreline benches on piedmont fans, incised alluvial-fan terrace risers / wash walls, and remanently magnetized Tertiary basalt flow margins beneath thin gravel.
Measured Outcome
Canary symmetric AUC = 0.636490 (≤ 0.90 PASS); free1d HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.008354 [0.006823, 0.010135] (+108.5% over R0, +38.2% over RELIEF p10, +52.2% over STRAINBREAK f60s2i); worst local 3-px overlap = 0.4605 (locally unique). Blocked from weekly slot only by the dense 17GEMSDOE public witness.

1. HYDROCONDUIT — Piedmont-Hinge Relative Monoclinal Scarp × Hydrothermal RTP Conduit

Layers Involved
Bands 12 (det_elev), 19 (det_elev_slope), 15 (depth_to_base_surf), 2 (rtp), 4 (geod_2ndinv), 8 (geod_dilaterate), 17 (cond_surf) + pinned geodawn_rad_u8.tif (K, Th) + pinned gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv (surface + quartz/chalcedony/Na-K-Ca geothermometers ≥80 °C).
Physical Signature
Background-normalized relative log-slope break ||grad log(slope+1°)|| × 600 m cross-strike step monotonicity μ_step (rejecting V-canyons) × piedmont/shallow-basement hinge × Andersonian strike/strain, corroborated by Laplace Band 2 RTP tilt-contact + demagnetization trough, K/(Th+1) potassic alteration, MT conductivity, and blind chemical geothermometer plumes.
Why Missing from Catalogue
Subtle 1–5 m Quaternary alluvial-fan scarps lack raw cliff relief and potential-field worms, and >75% of Great Basin geothermal upflow cools below 40 °C near surface while retaining silica/cation thermometry and magnetite destruction.
Distinction from Repo Lanes
Inverts BREAKLINE's 74.8% range-core cliff hijack to 83.4% piedmont / 6.2% range core, fixes the Band 6 tc = Radiometric Total Count mislabelling by using true Band 2 RTP, adds 419 blind geothermometer pixels, and unifies repacked H19-5 piedmont strands (55%) with 45–50% off-skeleton placement.
Named Non-Fault Mimic
Alluvial-fan coalescing bajada aprons, Pleistocene Lake Lahontan recessional shorelines at piedmont elevations, and altered volcanic margins / lateral thermal-outflow paleochannels.
Measured Outcome
Static HOLDOUT-DTI 0.008549 [0.007626, 0.009487] (+113.4% vs R0, +41.4% vs RELIEF p10); Canaries PASS (max AUC 0.6501 ≤ 0.90); Local overlap 0.5500 ≤ 0.70; Sparse public (≤150k px) 0/1,147 >70% (max 0.6719); Literal dense-surface stop triggers on 17GEMSDOE E-proba (verdict: negative, OK TO DOWNLOAD, DO NOT SUBMIT).

2. RTP-TILT-DEMAG — Reduced-to-Pole Laplace Tilt-Contact & Magnetite-Destruction Trough

Layers Involved
Band 2 (rtp), Band 17 (cond_surf), and geodawn_rad_u8.tif (K, Th).
Physical Signature
Miller–Singh / Verduzco phase-normalized RTP tilt-contact crest via Laplace's equation (&partial;²RTP/&partial;z² = -∇_H² RTP) + positive-Laplacian linear demagnetization troughs gated by K/(Th+1).

Distinction & Mimic
