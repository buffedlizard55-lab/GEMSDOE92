1. APRONRIDGE — Thin-Cover Piedmont Accommodation Apron & Inclination-Corrected RTP-Curvature 1D Ridges

Layers Involved
Band 15 (depth_to_base_surf), Band 12 (det_elev), Band 5 (iso_grav_anom_slope), Band 19 (det_elev_slope), Band 2 (rtp, Reduced-to-Pole magnetics), and Band 6 (tc, total curvature) — Bands 2, 6, and 15 were unused by all 13 prior built lanes.
Physical Signature
S_raw = H_apron(B12, B15 ~20–600 m log-normal, B5) × max(R_scarp[B19,B12], 0.85 × R_rtp[||∇B2|| × (-B6) × coh(B2)]) restricted to 4-axis 1D directional crests (directional_ridge_1d).
Why Missing from Catalogue
Synthetic hanging-wall step-faults and shallow-buried basement transfer faults in the thin-cover piedmont apron either offset homogeneous fan gravels (zero gravity worm) or have degraded surface scarps where raw TMI is laterally skewed 300–800 m by ~65° geomagnetic inclination; RTP centers the magnetic gradient directly over the vertical basement step.
Named Non-Fault Mimics
Sehoo/Eetza pluvial shoreline benches on piedmont fans, incised alluvial-fan terrace risers / wash walls, and remanently magnetized Tertiary basalt flow margins beneath thin gravel.
Measured Outcome
Canary symmetric AUC = 0.636490 (≤ 0.90 PASS); free1d HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.008354 [0.006823, 0.010135] (+108.5% over R0, +38.2% over RELIEF p10, +52.2% over STRAINBREAK f60s2i); worst local 3-px overlap = 0.4605 (locally unique). Blocked from weekly slot only by the dense 17GEMSDOE public witness.

Rank 2 · Screened & Folded into APRONRIDGE Channel 2

2. RTPTILT-CURV — Inclination-Corrected RTP Horizontal-Gradient × Inverted Curvature

Layers Involved
Band 2 (rtp) & Band 6 (tc)
Physical Signature
R_rtp = pct_rank(||∇rtp||) × pct_rank(-tc) × structure_tensor_coherence(rtp)
Why Missing from Catalogue
Recovers shallow-buried volcanic/crystalline fault contacts beneath coalescing bajadas without raw-TMI dipolar skew or elevation-dependent terrain correlation.
Named Non-Fault Mimics
Unfaulted pluton roof pendants, caldera ring-dike margins, and incised channels draining magnetic volcanics.
Screen Result
Canary AUC = 0.541297, off-skeleton SGMC = 0.034975; folded directly into APRONRIDGE where it wins 10.5% of far 1D crests.

Rank 3 · Screened · Reserve Hydrothermal Gate

3. BLIND-GEOTHERM — Sub-Boiling Geothermometer Upflow Halos on Thin-Cover Basement Steps

Layers Involved
gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv chemical geothermometers (na_k_ca_mg_c, quartz_c, chalcedony_c ≥ 90 °C; 477 cells vs 144 thermal vents) × Band 15 & Band 5
Physical Signature
Gaussian upflow halo (σ=18 px) around deep-equilibrated chemical geothermometer anomalies gating thin-cover gravity-gradient ridges.
Why Missing from Catalogue
Roughly 75% of Great Basin hydrothermal systems are blind (cool surface waters retaining >90 °C silica/cation equilibration signatures above unmapped permeable fault conduits).
Named Non-Fault Mimics
Lateral outflow plumes in regional carbonate aquifers and diagenetic silica enrichment in ash-rich lacustrine sediments.
Screen Result
Canary AUC = 0.551863, off-skeleton SGMC = 0.019012 (held in reserve due to uneven exploration-well sampling).
