## Method finding that outranks the artifact

Inside the H19-5 lineage the hide-and-recover holdout is **anti-correlated** with the reported
leaderboard score: Spearman `−0.905` (n = 8 measured files, per-dot holdout yield vs owner claim).
Mechanism: the instrument's truth is drawn *from* the mapped catalogue, while every top-scoring
submission deletes catalogue-adjacent dots — the two moves push in opposite directions.
Consequences for future sessions:

* A holdout win is **necessary but not sufficient**; do not use a holdout number as a score
  forecast, and do not gate on "beats the repository all-time holdout best" alone.
* Any future instrument must withhold truth that is **not** catalogue-derived, or correct for the
  catalogue-distance bias explicitly.

## Limitations and next steps

1. **No organizer receipt exists anywhere in this repo.** Every leaderboard number is a `CLAIM`.
2. **The literal duplicate rule cannot be satisfied.** 39 registry rasters carry more than
   150,000 positive pixels; their 3-px halos cover the footprint, so every non-empty candidate
   measures `1.000 > 0.70` (owner decision `D1`, `docs/REVIEW_20261030_NINESITE.md` §8). Only the
   owner can resolve this; nothing here relaxes it.
3. **H6 (per-dot yield pruning) is the highest-value untested experiment.** §3 shows that removing
   18,000 zero-yield dots from `h33` at constant coverage would move `0.2778 → ≈ 0.34`. It needs a
   calibrated per-dot probability, no new data.
4. **H7 (official 19-band stack) is blocked** by the missing DrivenData credentials; no public
   mirror with a matching SHA-256 exists in the 114 repositories scanned.
5. **The `MODEL` estimator is not trustworthy** (`valid_leaderboard_prediction: false`); until a
   receipt-bearing calibration set exists, ordering candidates by `MODEL` is advisory only.
6. **Redistribution terms of the externally sourced stacks are unverified**, and none of them has
   been confirmed as organizer-identical.
7. **User-supplied leaderboard snapshots `0.4413` / `0.3195` were not found** on the public top-22
   (2026-10-30 read: #1 `0.4544`). Treat them as stale.
