## Why GEMSDOE32 h33-h33-2-b2 (0.2778 CLAIM) scored highest — PhD-level analysis

Verified in `docs/BEAT_0_2778.md` and `docs/ANALYSIS_20261025_LISTRICLOQO.md` §1–2: R0 is the terminal thinning of the H19-5 multiline-corroborated worm skeleton:

* **H19-5** (19GEMSDOE, 121,131 px, 0.1922 CLAIM) → **D2.8** (GEMSDOE25, 44,090 px Poisson-thinned at 2.83 px ≈ 300 m, 0.2600 CLAIM) → **SOLO** (GEMSDOE28, 40,199 px with `XOR(SOLO,D2.8 & d_cat>1)==0`, 0.2708 CLAIM) → **R0 / GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2** (37,654 px with `XOR(R0,D2.8 & d_cat>2)==0`, 0.2778 CLAIM, zeros-outside encoding).

Mechanism: Under the distance-weighted Tversky DTI (α=0.2 FP, β=0.8 FN, 300 m triangular kernel), several nearby dots give overlapping `k(d)=max(1−d/3,0)` credit around one truth pixel. If TP_w per truth cell saturates at 1, removing redundant dots reduces weighted FP `α·(n−M)` while preserving most TP_w `T`, and a fixed budget `n=37,654` reallocated to uncovered ground can increase marginal coverage where `dT>α·DTI·(dT+1−k)`. Thinning in row-major scan order at the ~300 m correlation length therefore raised the claim score. The `d_cat>2` catalogue collar (≥300 m from USGS/INGENIOUS faults) also avoids penalizing catalogue-proximal predictions per the organizer's forum note that a dot near a known trace far from any new truth is fully penalized. No organizer receipt for 0.2778 exists in `ledger/submissions.csv`; it remains a **CLAIM**, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The current top-50 leaderboard snapshot (2026-10-25) lists **0.4413** (xiaofanhu) as #1, not a GEMSDOE entry; 0.3195 is not on that snapshot (see `docs/ANALYSIS_20261025_LEADERBOARD.md`).

**Can we beat 0.2778 with a unique TIF?** Yes in principle: R0 is 100% confined to H19-5 support and thinned blind to physics. A physics-informed reallocation of the same budget toward concealed Quaternary splays absent from H19-5—while preserving ~300 m spacing and the 2 px catalogue collar—can improve hidden-fault recovery on the holdout surrogate. Whether that lifts the hidden-test DTI above 0.2778 depends on the organizer's hidden truth geometry and cannot be projected from HOLDOUT-DTI alone; it must be verified by an organizer receipt.
