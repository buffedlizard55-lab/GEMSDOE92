## 4. Mathematical Specification of `RIEDELVENT` (`gems/riedelvent.py`)

### 4.1 En-Échelon Riedel $R$-Shear Obliquity (`riedel_obliquity_index`, `riedel_strain_coupling`)
Let $\theta_{\text{fine}}(x) \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ)$ denote the local axial strike orientation (with structure-tensor coherence $C_{\text{fine}}(x) \in [0, 1]$) of the fine-scale (`\sigma_{\text{fine}} = 1.5\text{ px} = 150\text{ m}`) composite surface/shallow-crustal break field (`3DEP 10 m` slope-p90 + `Band 2` RTP horizontal gradient), and let $\theta_{\text{master}}(x) \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ)$ denote the mesoscale (`\sigma_{\text{master}} = 14.0\text{ px} = 1.4\text{ km}`) axial strike orientation (with coherence $C_{\text{master}}(x) \in [0, 1]$) of the underlying basin-bounding master structure (`Band 18` gravity horizontal gradient + `Band 15` basement-depth step). The acute axial obliquity is:
$$\Delta\theta(x) = \arccos\!\bigl(|\cos(\theta_{\text{fine}}(x) - \theta_{\text{master}}(x))|\bigr) \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ]$$
Under Coulomb-Riedel brittle shear mechanics (Tchalenko, 1970) with internal friction angle $\phi \in [30^\circ, 38^\circ]$, synthetic $R$-shears form at $\Delta\theta_R = \phi/2 \in [15^\circ, 19^\circ]$ (with $P$-shears and low-angle relays spanning $[12^\circ, 28^\circ]$). We define the **En-Échelon Riedel Obliquity Index**:
$$I_{\text{Riedel}}(x) = \exp\!\left(-\frac{(\Delta\theta(x) - 19^\circ)^2}{2 \times (6.5^\circ)^2}\right) \cdot \sqrt{C_{\text{fine}}(x)\,C_{\text{master}}(x)}$$
and couple it to mesoscale (`4.5 km`) standardized geodetic shear and dilatation strain (`Bands 4, 7, 8`):
$$S_{\text{Riedel}}(x) = I_{\text{Riedel}}(x) \cdot \sigma_{\text{logistic}}\!\bigl(0.55\,z_{\text{meso}}(\dot{\varepsilon}_{\text{shear}}) + 0.45\,z_{\text{meso}}(\dot{\varepsilon}_{\text{dilat}})\bigr)$$

### 4.2 Hanging-Wall Flexural Hinge Curvature (`flexure_hinge_curvature`, `flexure_gravity_concordance`)
Above a concealed normal or listric fault, the hanging-wall basin fill exhibits a mesoscale ($500\text{–}1,500\text{ m}$) flexural rollover inflection. Let $B(x) = \log(1 + \max(Z_{\text{base}}(x), 0))$ (`Band 15`) and $G(x) = g_{\text{iso}}(x)$ (`Band 13`). Using a scale-normalized difference-of-Gaussians (`\sigma_1 = 4.5\text{ px} = 450\text{ m}`, `\sigma_2 = 13.5\text{ px} = 1,350\text{ m}`):
$$F_{\text{hinge}}(x) = \max\!\bigl(0,\, G_{\sigma_1} * B(x) - G_{\sigma_2} * B(x)\bigr) \cdot \|\nabla_{\sigma=2} B(x)\|$$
$$F_{\text{grav}}(x) = \max\!\bigl(0,\, G_{\sigma_2} * G(x) - G_{\sigma_1} * G(x)\bigr) \cdot \frac{\max(0, -\nabla B(x) \cdot \nabla G(x))}{\|\nabla B(x)\|\,\|\nabla G(x)\| + \varepsilon}$$
isolating basin-margin flexural hinges where basement deepening ($+\nabla B$) and gravity drop ($-\nabla G$) are antiparallel.

### 4.3 Slope-Regime Coupled 1D Crest Localizer (`build_riedelvent_crest_surface`)
Let $w_{\text{pied}}(x) = \exp(-(S_{100\text{m}}(x) / 9.5^\circ)^2) \in (0, 1]$ weight the low-slope piedmont/intrabasinal regime (`< 8°`) and $1 - 0.55\,w_{\text{pied}}(x)$ weight the steeper range-front/incised-knickzone regime. The unified 1D crest localizer combines four physical branches:
1. $L_{\text{scarp10m}}(x)$: USGS 3DEP `10 m` sub-pixel scarp excess, Wallace profile curvature, and linear scarp coherence (`Group A`);
2. $L_{\text{knick}}(x)$: `10 m` D8 channel $\chi$/$k_{sn}$ steps fused with `100 m` incised thalweg knickzone convexity $\lambda_1(z_{\text{det}}) \times \|\nabla\log(1+S_{100\text{m}})\|$ and cross-contour scarp transgression (`Group A + D`);
3. $L_{\text{riedel\_flex}}(x)$: En-échelon Riedel $12^\circ\text{–}28^\circ$ obliquity ridge + hanging-wall flexural hinge inflection (`Group D`);
4. $L_{\text{vent\_sub}}(x)$: Tufa-filtered INGENIOUS hydrothermal vent plume (`195` paleo-vents + `709` `2mDAB` probes + `286` thermal springs) $\times$ Grauch `TMI_up150 - RTP` demagnetization edge $\times$ Poisson magneto-gravity collinearity $\times$ gravity/basement tilt step (`Group B + C`).
