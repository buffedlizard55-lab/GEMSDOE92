## Required protocol if a lane is explicitly assigned later

1. **Stop condition first.** Obtain the exact authorized single-method paragraph. Resolve the literal registry witness and scope prospectively through an authorized protocol decision. Until then, do not select a hypothesis, generate a raster, or spend/select a weekly slot. Do not relax the 70%/3-px rule after a failure.
2. **Source and independence gate.** Pin each source URL, bytes, date/datum, CRS, footprint, resolution, licence, and checksum. Verify that the measurements are independent of the hidden target and the public fault catalogue. A landing page or an owner-mirror filename does not establish coverage or provenance.
3. **Preregister one mechanism and one non-fault mimic.** Use visible-fault information only for feature construction; never use withheld segments, hidden truth, or label-derived distance fields as predictors. Freeze a matched baseline, emission density, candidate count, and controls before touching holdout results.
4. **Feature-level leakage canaries.** Test every feature individually on the prescribed folds. If the absolute/two-polarity AUC exceeds **0.90**, stop and investigate leakage; do not continue by dropping a feature post hoc unless the preregistration explicitly permits it.
5. **Whole-segment buffered holdout.** Assign whole connected fault segments to spatially separated folds; buffer training away from held-out segments; exclude visible-fault cells from scoring with a pixel-exact mask. Use the shared evaluator and report the exact evaluator version, withheld-positive count, fold plan, and 95% paired spatial-block CI.
6. **Metric and controls.** Use pooled distance-weighted Tversky with **alpha = 0.2**, **beta = 0.8**, and the **300 m triangular kernel**, with a density-matched baseline/random control. Holdout DTI is a diagnostic—not an estimate of the organizer score.
7. **Pre- and post-placement registry gates.** Compare the continuous surface before placement and the final dot set afterward against the complete authorized registry. Stop on `|rho| > 0.90` or more than 70% of candidate dots within 3 pixels of a registry raster. No candidate may be called unique from a nine-file comparison alone.
8. **Budget and artifact discipline.** Maximum **3 experiments or 2 hours**, whichever comes first; stop at the first hard gate. Save a JSON run card with hypothesis, mechanism, mimic, holdout result and CI, registry comparisons, input/candidate SHA-256, validator output, submission name/note (note ≤140 characters), and promote/negative verdict. A negative result is a deliverable. No slot recommendation before positive, independent holdout evidence and all other gates pass.
9. **Format is separate.** For any future candidate, automatically verify the single-band float32 GeoTIFF against the template: CRS, shape, transform, all values in `[0,1]`, required outside-footprint encoding, mask, and catalogue-zero rules. A format PASS is not uniqueness, scientific promotion, organizer acceptance, or submission clearance.
10. **Owner-controlled submission only.** A separate human selector decides whether to use a weekly slot. Only an organizer-generated receipt can turn a score into **ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**.
