## 4. PhD-Level Analysis: Why `GEMSDOE32` Reached `0.2778` (`CLAIM`) and How to Beat It

### 4.1 Exact Metric Mechanics & Label-Generating Process
From the verified DOE/NLR *GEMS Prize Official Rules* (§2, Sept 2026, <https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf>) and `vendor/gems57/metric.py`, submissions are scored by the **pooled Distance-Tolerant Tversky Index** over `EPSG:32611` (`100 m` grid, $5,167,373$ active study-area cells):
$$\mathrm{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{\mathrm{TP}_w(P, G)}{\mathrm{TP}_w(P, G) + \alpha\,\mathrm{FP}_w(P, G) + \beta\,\mathrm{FN}_w(P, G)}, \qquad \alpha = 0.2,\;\beta = 0.8,\;K(d) = \max\!\left(0, 1 - \frac{d}{300\text{ m}}\right).$$
Crucially:
1. **Asymmetric `4:1` FN-to-FP penalty ($\beta/\alpha = 4$):** Missing a true unmapped fault pixel (`FN_w`) costs **four times** as much denominator weight as emitting an isolated false-positive dot (`FP_w = 0.2` per uncredited `1.0` pixel).
2. **Spatial kernel saturation vs. submodular dispersion:** Because $\mathrm{TP}_w$ saturates at `1.0` per ground-truth fault pixel via $\min(1, \dots)$ or distance-tolerant matching within $R = 3\text{ px}$ (`300 m`), clustering multiple `1.0` dots within `< 2.8 px` of one another adds zero marginal $\mathrm{TP}_w$ on covered truth while accumulating $\alpha = 0.2$ per redundant dot wherever truth is absent.
3. **Domain shift between `existing_faults.tif` and Phase 1 / Phase 2 ground truth:** Existing USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault pixels are excluded from scoring (<https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/2>), whereas Phase 1 ($50k, subset) and Phase 2 ($250k, full set) evaluate against **newly expert-identified faults** mapped by NLR/USGS geologists using **USGS GeoDAWN airborne magnetics/radiometrics (`doi:10.5066/P93LGLVQ`) and 1 m USGS 3DEP lidar** (Official Rules §2).
