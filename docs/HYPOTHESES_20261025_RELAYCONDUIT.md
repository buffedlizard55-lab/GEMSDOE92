## 3. Frozen Pre-Registered Specification for Selected Lane `RELAYCONDUIT`

### 3.1 Strict Leakage & Spatial Cross-Fitting Invariants (Protocol Rules 2 & 4)
1. **Visible-Only Fold Hygiene:** During `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` holdout (`seeds = (20, 30)` $\times$ 4 quadrants = 8 folds, `22,663` withheld positives), ALL fault-tip tangents, forward Coulomb relay cones, opposing-tip step-over bridges, and broadside stress-shadow indicators are computed **exclusively from `fold['visible']`** (`existing_faults \ hidden_draw`), with **exact 8-connected self-component exclusion** on every positive pixel so no training positive ever sees its own fault trace (`feature != label`).
2. **Pre-Fit Single-Feature Leakage Canary (Rule 4):** Every feature column (both zero-catalogue geophysical/geomorphic/hydrothermal channels and self-excluded tip-relay channels) is tested individually on the withheld holdout positives vs off-catalogue negatives **before** fitting any model. Hard fail-closed gate: $\max_j \text{AUC}_{\text{sym}}(f_j) \le 0.90$.
3. **Out-of-Fold Two-Stage Spatial Cross-Fitting:**
   - Outer split: Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (`quad in {0, 1, 2, 3}`), `12 px` (`1.2 km`) spatial guard band around the target quadrant, negative training samples restricted to `d_visible > 5.0 px` (`500 m`) and excluding `hidden_draw` dilated by `3.0 px` during holdout.
   - Stage 1 fits **two** regularized `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` models using internal 3-quadrant out-of-quadrant cross-prediction on the training quadrants (so Stage-1 predictions on the 3 training quadrants are strictly out-of-subfold, preventing Stage-2 overfitting to Stage-1 training residuals!):
     - $\mathcal{M}^{(1)}_{\text{blind}}$ (**Topography-Blind Subsurface & Hydrothermal Conduit Model**): uses ONLY subsurface gravity/basement/magnetic/MT/seismicity/strain/radiometric/INGENIOUS-vent channels (zero `B12`, zero `B19`, zero 3DEP DEM channels, zero catalogue channels).
     - $\mathcal{M}^{(1)}_{\text{phys}}$ (**Zero-Catalogue Multi-Physics + 3DEP-10m Scarp/$\chi$-Knickpoint Model**): uses all zero-catalogue physical, 3DEP-10m, $\chi$-knickpoint, seismicity, and hydrothermal channels (zero catalogue channels).
   - Stage-1 log-odds $\ell_{\text{blind}}, \ell_{\text{phys}}$ are propagated through **6-azimuth steerable line kernels** ($0^\circ, 30^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ, 120^\circ, 150^\circ$ at $\sigma_\parallel \in \{6, 18\}\text{ px}$, $\sigma_\perp = 1.5\text{ px}$) to compute continuous along-strike structural support and axial bridge continuity without touching any test-quadrant labels.
   - Stage 2 cross-fits $\mathcal{M}^{(2)}_{\text{blind}}$ (topography-blind + tip-relay + blind OOF auto-context) and $\mathcal{M}^{(2)}_{\text{full}}$ (full stack + tip-relay + OOF auto-context), combined via sedimentary-cover weight $w_{\text{cover}}(\mathbf{x}) = \text{clip}\!\bigl(\log(1 + \max(B_{15}, 0)) / \log(1 + 500),\, 0.22,\, 0.72\bigr)$.
