# Pre-registered hypotheses — 2026-10-25 `PHASEWIND` lane

This document was written before computing a new candidate surface, fitting a new model, or placing candidate dots. It ranks five ideas that are **not implemented/evaluated in the checked-out `gems/` code**. Several were previously mentioned as future ideas in review notes; they are not represented as novel across the broader GEMSDOE sites. No organizer receipt or leaderboard score is inferred from any hypothesis.

## Scope, source availability, and limits

- **Selected lane:** `PHASEWIND` — scale-persistent complex-wavelet phase winding around terminations of RTP magnetic contacts. No blend with another lane is permitted in this experiment.
- **Budget:** at most three pre-registered arms and two hours. No competition upload or weekly slot is authorized here.
- **Data:** Band 2 (`rtp`, reduced-to-pole magnetic anomaly) from the 19-band `training_features.tif` stack; shared grid/sample and mapped-fault catalogue from `data/bridge/`. The stack is the competition feature product and is pinned in `registry/session_manifest.json`; this checkout obtains it through a SHA-256-verified owner-repository mirror. The manifest explicitly says its organizer provenance and redistribution terms are **not independently verified**. The official competition data tab was live-checked and redirected to login, so its package bytes/licensing were not independently verified from the organizer. No new external dataset is required for the selected hypothesis.
- **Holdout:** `vendor/gems57/holdout.py` and `vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py`; buffered whole connected components; features in this lane do not use the catalogue, so there are no candidate-side catalogue features to reconstruct. Fold-visible distance controls emission (`d_visible > 2 px`) and the shared evaluator masks visible catalogue pixels exactly. Report only `HOLDOUT-DTI`, evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI; it is not a competition score.
- **Leakage canary:** test every candidate feature independently against withheld positives and sampled far negatives using symmetric AUC. A value above 0.90 is treated as leakage until explained; do not combine a failed feature into a trusted model.

## Frozen `PHASEWIND` arms, feasibility amendment, and gates

Initial arms written before surface computation:

1. `phasewind_200_400`: same-sign, nonzero winding at σ = 2 px and σ = 4 px.
2. `phasewind_400_800`: same-sign, nonzero winding at σ = 4 px and σ = 8 px.
3. `phasewind_200_400_800`: same-sign winding at any two of σ = 2/4/8 px (primary).

All were initially assigned the pinned parent's 37,654-dot budget and 2.83-px spacing. **Pre-DTI feasibility amendment (after feature canaries, before any HOLDOUT-DTI evaluation):** the shared spaced emitter returned a maximum of 18,789 dots for arm 1, 3,411 for arm 2, and 21,310 for the primary arm on seed 20's fold-visible domain (seed 30 primary capacity 21,291). A 37,654-dot output would require zero-score filler or a different spacing rule, neither allowed by the protocol. Therefore only the pre-registered primary arm will be scored at a fixed 20,000-dot budget, below its measured capacity on both seeds; the two ablations are not scored. This unplanned capacity amendment uses no holdout score to set the budget; it changes the emission budget and must be disclosed, so an apparent DTI advantage cannot be described as like-for-like with the 37,654-dot best.

**Implementation-equivalence note:** the code uses unscaled first derivatives rather than explicitly multiplying by σ. That positive per-scale scalar leaves phase winding unchanged; amplitude support is ranked within each scale and the numerical floor scales with its median, so the resulting feature is invariant to the omitted multiplier.

The primary uses 2.83-px spacing, catalogue clearance `d_cat > 2 px` for deployment, and fold-visible `d_visible > 2 px` for holdout. No catalogue-derived input enters the phase surface. Every per-scale feature and each arm surface is tested by the leakage canary before interpreting DTI.

**Decision rule:** `promote` is impossible without a strict `HOLDOUT-DTI` point estimate above the documented repository best (`COULOMBLOQO standalone`, 0.113316 on the strict `d_visible > 2 px` instrument), evidence that the gain is not a fold/feature leakage artifact, and a complete uniqueness scan against the accessible registry. Because the amended arm has 20,000 rather than 37,654 dots, any cross-budget comparison remains descriptive and cannot itself clear promotion. The separate selector owns weekly-slot promotion. Any failed gate yields `negative`. If the literal registry rule triggers, stop before full-domain dot placement and do not package a candidate TIFF; a research TIFF is not an exemption from the uniqueness gate.
