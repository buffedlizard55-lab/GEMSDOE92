## 1. Mathematical Anatomy of the DrivenData DOE GEMS Distance-Tolerant IoU (`DTI`) Metric

Let $\Omega \subset \{1,\dots,3730\}\times\{1,\dots,3292\}$ denote the active `5,167,373`-pixel Great Basin footprint (`100 m × 100 m` cells in `EPSG:32611`), $C \subset \Omega$ the `60,988`-pixel mapped fault compilation (`existing_faults.tif`, masked to zero before scoring), and $T^{\star} \subset \Omega \setminus C$ the hidden unmapped Quaternary/geothermal fault pixel set of total mass $G = |T^{\star}|$.

With the `300 m` (`3.0 px`) linear triangular distance kernel $K_r(d) = \max(0,\, 1 - d / 3.0)$, a candidate raster $p : \Omega \setminus C \to [0, 1]$ generates:
1. **Predicted coverage field** $C_p(x) = \max_{y \in \Omega \setminus C} p(y)\,K_r(\|x - y\|_2)$, so **true-positive mass** is:
   $$\text{TP}_w(p) = \sum_{x \in T^{\star}} C_p(x)$$
2. **False-positive mass** weighted against true coverage $C_{T^{\star}}(y) = \max_{x \in T^{\star}} K_r(\|y - x\|_2)$:
   $$\text{FP}_w(p) = \sum_{y \in \Omega \setminus C} p(y)\,\big(1 - C_{T^{\star}}(y)\big)$$
3. **Asymmetric Distance-Tolerant IoU** (`alpha = 0.2`, `beta = 0.8`):
   $$\text{DTI}(p) = \frac{\text{TP}_w(p)}{0.2\,\text{TP}_w(p) + 0.8\,G + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w(p)}$$

Taking the Fréchet / marginal pixel increment $\Delta \text{DTI}(y)$ when setting pixel $y$ from $0 \to 1$ at current score level $D = \text{DTI}(p)$ yields the **exact marginal optimality threshold**:
$$\Delta \text{DTI}(y) > 0 \iff \frac{\Delta \text{TP}_w(y)}{\Delta \text{FP}_w(y)} > \frac{0.2\,D}{1 - 0.2\,D}$$
At $D = 0.2784$, the break-even marginal ratio is:
$$\tau^{\star}(0.2784) = \frac{0.2 \times 0.2784}{1 - 0.2 \times 0.2784} = \mathbf{0.05896}$$

Three structural consequences follow immediately from $\tau^{\star} \approx 0.059$:
1. **Why Continuous Probability Surfaces Collapse (`0.0206 – 0.1647` `CLAIM`):** Any raster that emits unthresholded background probability $p(y) \in (0.01, 0.25)$ across millions of basin/range cells (`GEMSDOE28 s18-mxtp: 0.0206`, `GEMSDOE36 submission_p97: 0.1647`, `17GEMSDOE E-proba-multiscale: 0.1560`) accumulates $10^5$–$10^6$ units of $0.2\,\text{FP}_w$ denominator drag without increasing $\max_y p(y) K_r(\|x-y\|)$ on fault corridors already covered.
2. **Why `2.5–3.0 px` Sparse Crest Dotting Gives a `1.44×` Boost (`0.1922 -> 0.2778` `CLAIM`):** A continuous 1-px-wide skeleton line places 3–5 adjacent `1.0` pixels inside a single `3 px` (`300 m`) kernel disk. The second, third, and fourth pixels add almost **zero** marginal $\Delta \text{TP}_w$ (because $\max_y K_r$ is submodular and already saturated near $1.0$), while paying **full linear** $0.2\,(1 - C_{T^{\star}})$ false-positive cost! Thinning a line to isolated `1.0` dots spaced by `2.6–3.0 px` preserves $>91\%$ of corridor coverage $\text{TP}_w$ while cutting $\text{FP}_w$ by $\sim 60$–$70\%$.
3. **Why Near-Catalogue Collar Pruning (`d_cat > 2–4 px`) Helps:** Cells at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$ (`200 m`) sit inside the digitization uncertainty halo of already-masked `existing_faults.tif` traces; unmapped *new* strands overwhelmingly occupy synthetic/antithetic piedmont step-overs, relay ramps, and intra-basin grabens at $d_{\text{cat}} > 3$–$15\text{ px}$.
