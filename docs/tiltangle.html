Hypothesis & Miller–Singh (1994) tilt-angle audit

The USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue is a surface-scarp catalogue: faults are mapped where a preserved topographic scarp exists. A blind normal fault buried under thin Quaternary cover, or one whose scarp has diffused below detection, still juxtaposes rock bodies of different density across a steep plane.

The TILTANGLE surface computes theta = atan2(|dg/dz|, |grad_h g|) from bands B11 (vertical derivative), B13 (gravity anomaly, differentiated for N–S derivative), and B18 (E–W horizontal derivative), and sets S_raw = hgm * cos(theta)^2 = hgm^3 / (hgm^2 + vg^2). Per Miller & Singh (1994, J. Appl. Geophys. 32:213–217), directly over a steep vertical density contact dg/dz = 0 while |grad_h g| peaks, so theta -> 0 deg and cos(theta)^2 -> 1; over flat interiors where |grad_h g| -> 0 and |dg/dz| > 0, theta -> 90 deg and cos(theta)^2 -> 0 (correcting the inverted prose in the initial docstring). While the pure surface S is catalogue-free (max canary symmetric AUC 0.526594 <= 0.90), multiplying S by d_pref computed from the full existing_faults.tif catalogue leaked withheld fault locations into static scoring (symmetric AUC 0.962399 > 0.90).

Named non-fault mimic: equant plutonic intrusions, circular playa/evaporite density bodies, and non-fault lithologic density contacts.
