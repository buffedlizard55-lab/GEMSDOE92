It could also hurt if removed dots would have been near unseen faults. Hidden labels and a matched, density-controlled ablation tied to an organizer receipt are absent; proximity to mapped faults does not prove a prediction was false.

  2. Multi-temporal thermal/vegetation anomalies: persistent line-shaped thermal or phenology contrasts tied to fluid pathways; mimic: lithology, soil moisture, irrigation, elevation/aspect, and land-cover boundaries. Medium-high processing cost; time series and coverage not checked.
  3. Observed groundwater-head phase/gradient discontinuities: wells across structural blocks showing a repeatable hydraulic barrier or conduit response; mimic: pumping, seasonal recharge, screened-depth differences, and basin-fill heterogeneity. Sparse-data and temporal confounding risks; source bytes/coverage unverified.

Hydrochemical/isotope mixing fronts: independent multi-analyte source-tracer transitions consistent with deep-fluid mixing along concealed structures; mimic: evaporation, lithology/weathering, irrigation return, contamination, and aquifer-depth effects.
